# Company Context

## Working definition
Khatim is intended to build trusted digital transactions: signing, sealing, authentication, verification, identity and cryptographic infrastructure.

## Strategic context
The brand should be capable of serving both the visible user experience (send, sign, verify) and the invisible infrastructure (certificates, keys, trust lists, timestamps, APIs, HSMs, policy, audit evidence).

## Key tension
The product can be technically deep while the brand experience must remain understandable to legal, HR, procurement, executives and everyday signers.

## Strategic implication
Brand architecture should allow “Khatim” to operate as a platform/company name, with product names underneath rather than using the company name as a synonym for one signature workflow.
