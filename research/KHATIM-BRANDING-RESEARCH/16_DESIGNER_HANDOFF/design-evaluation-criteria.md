# Design Evaluation Criteria

Score future concepts from 1–5 on:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Distinctiveness | Would this still stand out beside 10 trust/e-signature brands? |
| Strategic fit | Does the form express the chosen positioning, not just the category? |
| Cultural intelligence | Is Arabic heritage meaningful rather than decorative? |
| Scalability | Does it survive expansion into identity, verification and APIs? |
| Bilingual strength | Does it work in Arabic and Latin? |
| Trust credibility | Does it feel serious without feeling generic? |
| Digital performance | Is it legible from favicon to dashboard? |
| Ownability | Can the system produce recognizable assets beyond the logo? |
| Longevity | Will it feel appropriate as technology and regulation evolve? |
