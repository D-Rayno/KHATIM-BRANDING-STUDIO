# Archetype Analysis

### Ruler
Useful for authority and officiality; risk of institutional coldness.

### Sage
Useful for precision and trust; risk of technical distance.

### Guardian
Useful for security; risk of generic cybersecurity codes.

### Magician
Useful for transformation from physical to digital trust; risk of hype.

### Recommended archetypal blend
**Sage + Guardian + Ruler**, with a small “Magician” impulse for transformation. This blend supports enterprise credibility without becoming a generic cybersecurity brand.
