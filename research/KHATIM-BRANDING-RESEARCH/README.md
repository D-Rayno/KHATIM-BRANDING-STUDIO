# KHATIM — Branding Research & Strategy Package

**Research cut-off:** 26 August 2026

This package is a strategic discovery document for Khatim (خاتم) before final identity design. It intentionally **does not finalize a logo, font, palette, icon set or visual system**.

## The most important finding

The exact name **Khatim** is already used in the digital-trust / PKI / digital-signature market by Codegic: KhatimDoc, Khatim Sign Server, Khatim PKI Server, Khatim Timestamp Server and Khatim Verification Server are publicly marketed products. The PKI Consortium also lists multiple Khatim products from Codegic. This is a material naming and trademark/domain risk, not a cosmetic issue. It must be addressed before significant investment in identity design.

## Strategic conclusion in one sentence

The strongest brand opportunity is likely **not “a prettier digital seal”** but a brand that owns the idea of **making digital intent count: the moment identity, evidence, agreement and integrity become final and trustworthy** — while preserving the cultural depth of خاتم and allowing expansion into trust infrastructure.

## Evidence hierarchy

Regulators, standards bodies, official company material and museum/academic resources are primary. Branding-agency articles are secondary and are flagged as such.

## What remains intentionally undecided

- final name vs. possible name modification
- final logo concept
- final color palette
- final typefaces
- exact tagline
- precise product architecture
- whether Khatim is positioned first as e-signature, digital trust, identity, or an infrastructure layer

## Designer usage

Treat `13_CREATIVE_TERRITORIES` as exploration prompts, not concepts to copy. The final identity should emerge from a chosen strategic position, customer evidence and name-clearance findings.
