# Industry Overview

## The market in one model

**Identity → authentication → intent/approval → signature/seal → timestamp → validation → evidence → preservation → future verification.**

The market has historically separated these capabilities across e-signature platforms, PKI/CA providers, identity vendors and document systems. Regulation and platform evolution are gradually pulling them closer together.

## What changed

- eSignature became mainstream workflow software.
- High-assurance digital signatures added cryptographic evidence.
- Trust-service regimes formalized levels of assurance.
- Digital identity wallets are becoming a distribution layer for credentials and signing.
- Verifiable credentials broaden the concept from documents to machine-verifiable claims.

## Strategic consequence
The category is converging around a larger problem: **how do two parties know that a digital action came from the right identity, with the intended meaning, and remained intact?**
