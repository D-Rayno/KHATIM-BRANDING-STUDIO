# Industry Evolution

### Phase 1 — Physical authority
Seal, stamp, signed paper, notary, physical presence.

### Phase 2 — Digital document workflow
Scan, email, PDF, basic e-signature.

### Phase 3 — Cryptographic evidence
Certificates, PKI, digital signatures, timestamps, validation.

### Phase 4 — Digital trust platforms
Identity verification, e-seals, remote signing, evidence, APIs, compliance.

### Phase 5 — Portable trust
Digital identity wallets, verifiable credentials, attestations and cross-system trust.

**Strategic hypothesis:** Khatim should be branded for Phase 4→5, even if its first product launches in Phase 3/4.
