# Competitor Positioning

## Observable positioning patterns

**Workflow players:** emphasize ease, speed, completion and productivity.

**Security players:** emphasize high assurance, compliance, PKI, identity and fraud prevention.

**Sovereign players:** emphasize regulatory status, local infrastructure, data control and legal validity.

**Infrastructure players:** emphasize integration, standards, HSMs, APIs and deployment control.

## Whitespace

Khatim can potentially own a sentence structure like:

> **A trusted digital action is not just signed; it is made attributable, intact and final.**

This bridges the workflow and infrastructure ends of the market.
