# Competitor Messaging

## Repeated language
- secure
- trusted
- compliant
- legally binding
- simple / easy
- fast
- digital transformation
- automate workflows
- identity verification
- fraud prevention
- protect documents

## What these messages do
They reduce perceived risk but often fail to create distinctive meaning. “Secure and compliant” is a qualification criterion; it is rarely an owned idea.

## Khatim implication
Move from **security claim** to **meaningful outcome**: officiality, proof, finality, provenance, trusted completion, or institutional certainty.
