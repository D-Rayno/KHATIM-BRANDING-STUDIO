# Competitor Matrix

See [`competitor-matrix.csv`](competitor-matrix.csv) for structured data.

## Summary

- **DocuSign:** strongest category recognition; increasingly moving into agreement intelligence.
- **Adobe:** exceptional document/PDF ecosystem and trust ecosystem.
- **Dropbox Sign:** simplicity and accessibility.
- **PandaDoc:** document-to-deal workflow and brandability.
- **OneSpan:** security depth + eSignature + IDV, strong regulated-sector fit.
- **Entrust:** infrastructure, PKI, HSM, identity and country-scale trust.
- **Universign/Docaposte:** sovereign/regulatory credibility with European roots.
- **emdha/Signit:** strong local Saudi trust and regulatory relevance.
- **Ethaq Plus/Dubai PKI:** government-backed UAE trust infrastructure.
- **Codegic Khatim:** exact naming overlap across PKI/signing/timestamp/verification.

The final competitor set for visual benchmarking should be narrowed to 12–15 websites and audited screen-by-screen before design begins.
