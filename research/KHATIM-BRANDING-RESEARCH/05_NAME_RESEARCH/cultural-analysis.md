# Cultural Analysis

Historical Islamic seals were used to connect people to manuscripts and objects through identifiable impressions; museum and manuscript resources show seals as evidence of ownership/provenance. [S44][S45][S46]

This supports a culturally meaningful narrative: **a mark is valuable because it carries identity and evidence.**

Avoid treating “Islamic” as a decorative style. The cultural contribution should be conceptual: identity, authorship, authority, provenance, continuity and the legitimacy of a mark.
