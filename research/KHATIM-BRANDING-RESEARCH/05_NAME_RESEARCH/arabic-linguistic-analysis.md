# Arabic Linguistic Analysis

The Arabic Academy source is particularly useful because it anchors the word in the root ختم. The root includes: to stamp/impress with a seal, to seal/close, and to complete or reach the end. The noun خاتم is associated with a ring/seal. [S42][S43]

### Strategic semantic field
**seal — imprint — closure — completion — finality — identity — trace — authority**

### Important nuance
Khatim is not equivalent to the Arabic word for “signature” in a strict lexical sense. Its power lies in the broader semantic relationship between a mark, a seal and completion. This distinction is strategically valuable.
