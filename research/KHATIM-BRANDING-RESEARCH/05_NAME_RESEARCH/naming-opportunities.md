# Naming Opportunities

## Route A — Keep Khatim
Best when clearance is clean enough and differentiation can be protected through corporate scope, geography or brand architecture.

## Route B — Khatim + distinctive descriptor
Could create separation while preserving the Arabic asset, but the exact Khatim term may still create confusion.

## Route C — derive a new name from the same semantic field
Potential territories: imprint, proof, closure, attestation, mark, witness, covenant, trace. This is the safer strategic fallback if Khatim cannot be cleared.

## Route D — corporate brand vs. product brand separation
Use a distinct corporate identity with Khatim as a product name only if legal and market architecture allow it.

These are routes for naming exploration, not final names.
