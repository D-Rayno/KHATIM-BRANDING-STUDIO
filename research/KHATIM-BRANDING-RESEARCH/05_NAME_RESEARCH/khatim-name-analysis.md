# Khatim Name Analysis

## Semantic strengths
**خاتم** is compact, memorable and deeply connected to the root ختم. The Arabic Language Academy links the term to ring/seal meanings, while the root includes stamping/impressing and completion. [S42][S43]

## Strategic strengths
- Ownable narrative beyond “signature.”
- Short and globally pronounceable with moderate effort.
- Strong connection between identity and marking.
- Can stretch into seals, verification, identity and evidence.

## Strategic weakness
The word may be interpreted internationally as “ring” or as an unfamiliar Arabic name before the category is known. That is manageable through story and positioning.

## Critical issue
An exact same-category technology product family already uses Khatim. [S38][S39][S40][S41]
