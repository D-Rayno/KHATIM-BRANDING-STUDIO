# Purchase Objections

1. “Are you legally recognized here?”
2. “Can we trust the cryptography and keys?”
3. “Can this integrate with our ERP/CRM/HSM/identity stack?”
4. “Where is our data stored?”
5. “Will the evidence still verify years later?”
6. “How does this work across borders?”
7. “What happens if a certificate is revoked or expires?”
8. “Is this just another e-signature SaaS product?”

The brand should answer these with architecture, evidence and governance—not slogans.
