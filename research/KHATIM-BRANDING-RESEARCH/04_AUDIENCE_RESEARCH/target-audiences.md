# Target Audiences

## Primary
**Enterprise decision makers:** CIO/CTO, CISO, legal, compliance, procurement, HR, operations.

**Regulated organizations:** banks, insurance, healthcare, government, public-sector bodies.

**Platform builders:** product/engineering teams embedding signing, verification or identity into applications.

## Secondary
SMEs, professional services, legal departments, real estate, education and individual professionals.

The brand must reassure the technical evaluator while remaining understandable to the business buyer and signer.
