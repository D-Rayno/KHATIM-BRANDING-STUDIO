- Strategic differentiation — 20%
- Strategic fit — 20%
- Memorability — 15%
- Credibility — 10%
- Cultural relevance — 10%
- International scalability — 10%
- Future expansion — 10%
- Digital usability — 5%

Activate a Brand Critic persona/agent to challenge every territory. ### R5. Synthesis and Recommendation
Activate a Creative Director persona/agent to synthesize the critique and recommend the top 3 territories to carry forward.
