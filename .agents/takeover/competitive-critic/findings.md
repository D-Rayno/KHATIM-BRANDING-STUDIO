## 1. Geometry — what the four files actually are

All four constructions share one grammar: **one filled diamond unit among outlined diamond units, on a 45° axis.**

| File | Construction | Raster read |
|---|---|---|
| **master** (256) | A large open **rhombic lattice** (~8×8 cells, 3px thin strokes, one filled 28px cell near center at 130,130) | A **grid/pixel-tessellation** fills the whole field; the single filled cell is a minor pattern-interruption buried inside a dominant lattice. |
| **compact** (128) | A **2×2 pinwheel** of 4 large open diamonds (44px) + one solid center diamond | Reads as a tight **geometric four-point star / diamond monogram** with a center dot — NOT a grid. Sharp, iconic. |
| **negative** (256) | Solid field, 4 cells knocked out, one filled cell re-asserted | Generic high-contrast cutout mark; least distinctive. |
| **16px** (16) | 4 filled diamonds + stroke-outlined center | A star/pinwheel with an open center = cancelled event. |

**The two leading candidates are in tension.**
- The **master** preserves the displacement idea (one cell changed out of many) but is a **grid** — exactly the "pixel-grid trope" the team flagged as kill-condition #1 (`05-ADVERSARIAL-QA.md`).
- The **compact** kills the grid but **destroys the displacement event** — a filled center reads as a generic focal dot, and the mark collapses into the commodity "geometric tech diamond/star." The semantic that made the idea special evaporates.

**16px is inverted logic:** filled field with an *empty* center = the "absence" is now the exception, inverted from the master's filled exception. The responsive chain does not keep one concept — it flips it. This is a system incoherence, not an insurance form.

## 2. Collision map (live, 2025/2026)

**A. "Pixel-grid / one-changed-cell" — the DIRECT and now NAMED collision.**
LogoLounge's **2025 Logo Trend Report names this literal gesture "PolyGrid"**: *grids of cells... shake up expectations by dropping in a wild mix of forms... order and individuality living side by side.* Khatim's master IS a PolyGrid mark. The "pixel-art revival" is separately documented as a 2025 trend. The team's internal kill condition ("reads primarily as a generic pixel/grid technology logo") is **now independently true of the master.** Phase 4 did NOT resolve the trope — it re-issued it in diamond form, which does not escape the grid reading: a diamond lattice is still a lattice.

**B. QR-reflection (VERIFICATION-CATEGORY SPECIFIC — the most damaging).**
A filled module inside a regular matrix IS the visual grammar of a QR code. In the exact category Khatim sells (verification / digital-trust), the master's filled-cell-in-matrix will read as a **QR fragment / data-module**. Compounding: rotated/"diamond" QR presentation is itself a live design move in 2026 ("square to diamond" QR styling). Khatim cannot own "one changed block in a matrix" as verification-proof; QR already owns "matrix encodes truth."

**C. DocuSign Nexus 2024 (the market leader).**
The Nexus logomark itself is two overlapping shapes (a D + an agreement) — not a pixel grid. **But** DocuSign's entire *brand-system element language* is doctrinally built on the **45° diagonal + basic geometric cells**: pictograms constructed on a 32px grid, "any angle should run along 45°," isometric illustration grids. So the **45°-diagonal-geometric-cell register is now claimed by the market leader as its system grammar** — including in native use as decorative field textures (exactly the master's lattice). Khatim's "decisive 45° nuqta field" is navigating inside DocuSign-owned ship-lanes.

**D. Isometric/cube/diamond "tech" glyphs — saturated commodity.**
"Abstract isometric cube / diamond" is a **cheap vector-template genre** (design.com, logowik, Creative Market, all tagged for *security / blockchain / AI / analytics*). The compact star/diamond drifts directly into this sea. Nothing in F-02-style compact construction clears it.

**E. Blockchain/ledger.**
"One changed node/block in a distributed field" is the crypto stable. A filled die among a lattice triggers blockchain/ledger/module association (and the compact star touches ethereum-ish hex/diamond territory).

**F. Generic geometric SaaS.**
The 2026 landscape explicitly warns: geometry is dominant but *differentiation now comes from unexpected proportion/meaning, not bare geometry*. A clean diamond star is precisely the "geometric combination mark" the entire category can produce without detection.

**G. Independent fatal precedent found (Pixelmojo).**
An existing brand system is built entirely on **"four cells, one active verb cell"** — the *same conceptual skeleton* as Khatim's displacement, deployed as a coherent multi-product grammar. This is independent prior art of the idea. Khatim's "one filled unit = the event" is **not a novel concept in the market; it is a trend with at least one shared owner and a PolyGrid name.**

## 3. Differentiation — score

**Score: 3.5 / 10**, and the reason is the two-candidate split:

- MASTER: ideas-grade differentiation driven by *system* (displacement semantics) — **but** on raw *form* it lands in collision (A + B + C). As a logo glyph it is under-differentiated ("one of many geometric tech grids").
- COMPACT: form is cleaner and more ownable as a glyph — **but** it has shed the differentiating idea and drifts into commodity diamond/star (D + F), and its "filled center" is a generic focal dot.

**Net: the mark does not currently OWN a read.** It trades between "direct collision" (master) and "generic glyph" (compact) and belongs to neither defensible lane.

**It is "one of many geometric tech glyphs"** — the master is one of many geometric grids; the compact is one of many diamond-stars. Differentiation exists only as *intended semantics*, not as *readable visual territory*.

---

## 4. Copyability & defensibility

**Copyable now, with zero detection:** Yes. The PolyGrid/pixel-grid register is so saturated that a competitor agency (DocuSign next IAM refresh, any verification startup, any PKI SaaS) could set "one filled cell among outlined cells on a 45° grid" tomorrow and nobody could raise a hand — it is a *named trend*, not trade dress.

**Defensibility moat exists ONLY at system level, not form level.** The form (a filled diamond in a lattice) is commodity. What is *slowly* defensible is the **system**: the responsive chain (full-field → compact → negative → 16px), the fixed 45° nuqta discipline, the single-verb motion, the tripartite evidence language. But — see §2G — even the *concept* now has prior art; a system moat protects execution, not territory.

**Does "one filled among outlined" hold as trade dress?** **No.** It is a commodity gesture. Ultra-minimal geometries register far too narrowly for broad claim, are difficult to enforce, and the fill-vs-outline dual-state is common stock (toggle/active-element states exist throughout UI). The earlier Phase 3 note that F-02 is "defensible only through proportion/system discipline" is correct — and that discipline is exactly what is not yet proven or locked.

## 5. Whitespace — is ABSENCE the open lane?

The Phase 3 competitive agent's core claim survives scrutiny and is *the only genuinely open lane in this landscape*: **every major competitor marks PRESENCE — strokes, swooshes, shields, particles, pixels — nobody marks ABSENCE as the subject.**

The evidence confirms this: Cluster A (agreement SaaS) marks presence with pens/squiggles; Cluster B (PKI/cyber) with shield-checks/orbits; DocuSign with a filled convergence shape; the whole category with filled modules. **No one builds the mark on the void, the impression, the thing that is NOT there after a change.**

But — and this is the critique's point — **Phase 4 did not enter that lane.** It stacked presence (a lattice of strokes / a field of cells) and filled one of them. That is a PRESENCE mark with a highlighted member — the opposed, occupied register. The negative-space file is the only one that even touches the void lane, and it too re-fills the center with solid, retreating to presence.

**The rebuild direction:** make the *absence* the subject — a field defined by what is missing after a decisive event; the event as a physical impression (F-05 Void Core / F-16 Indent lineage), not as a filled cell among outlined cells. "One filled among many" is a presence taxonomy; "one absence in a field of permanence" is the open, uncopied whitespace.

## 6. Overall

**Does this mark carve clear competitive territory, or blend into the category?**

It **blends**. The master blends into the geometric-grid/pixel/QR register (occupied and trending); the compact blends into the generic diamond-star monogram register (occupied and template-cheap). Both are specimens of the saturated "geometric tech" look the 2026 landscape itself warns against. The single sharp idea — a permanent, irreversible change — is real and valuable, but the *chosen visual resolution* (fill a cell in a lattice) is the one resolution most likely to collide, and the alternative resolution (pinwheel) is the one most likely to vanish into commodity.

**Primary claim of this audit:** Phase 4 **worsened** a live risk by keeping "filled cell in a field" (the pixel-grid trope) while adding a nameable trend collision (PolyGrid) and a category-fatal QR read — and its "fix" (compact) discards the very idea that justified the risk.

---

## VERDICT

**REBUILD.**

- **Keep the idea:** "one permanent change among many" — the displacement event. It is the brand's best thesis and is not shared as a positioning.
- **Keep the system discipline** (responsive chain, 45° nuqta reference, single-verb motion, monochrome-first) — that discipline is the only latent moat.
- **Abandon the FORM** of "filled cell among outlined cells." It is a named 2025 PolyGrid trend, a QR-fragment read in the verification category, adjacent to market-leader DocuSign's cell-grid doctrine, and impossible to defend as trade dress.
- **Re-enter the open lane: absence/impression/negative-space** (F-05/F-16 lineage) — the one register no major competitor occupies. Make the *missing* unit the subject, and keep the *form* so distinctive it cannot be "one more geometric tech glyph."

**Do NOT ship the current master or compact as the identity.** Both are DEVELOMENT-DEAD as logomark candidates on differentiation-copyability grounds, independent of the (still-valid) rendering/IP/legal gates in `06-PHASE-4-DEVELOPMENT-VERDICT.md`.
