## 2. Logic Chain
- **Step 1:** The mandate requires evaluating whether territories are genuinely defensible, name-independent, free of cybersecurity and orientalist clichés, and grounded in product reality.
- **Step 2:** A territory that can be visually mimicked by DocuSign IAM or Adobe in 30 days provides zero commercial value (disqualifying T-01).
- **Step 3:** A territory that is 100% invisible or completely discards the project's cultural narrative moat surrenders Khatim's primary strategic whitespace (disqualifying T-05 and T-08 as standalone brands).
- **Step 4:** A territory that is too heavy, cold, or sovereign-focused creates an unbridgeable MVP reality gap for a commercial SaaS launch (disqualifying T-03 and T-07 as standalone brands).
- **Step 5:** The remaining contenders—**T-02 (The Witness Mark)**, **T-06 (Proof / Evidence)**, and **T-04 (Digital Heritage)**—form a mutually reinforcing triad:
  - T-02 provides the **Institutional and Legal Anchor** (Tripartite Attestation & Archival Imprint).
  - T-06 provides the **Commercial and Operational Engine** (Forensic Truth, Self-Authenticating Court Packets).
  - T-04 provides the **Cultural & Visual Moat** (The Mathematical Signet Bezel & $45^\circ$ Nuqta Grid).
- **Step 6:** Deprioritized territories donate their best functional subsystems (T-01 container states, T-05 JSON ledger trays, T-08 passkey latency telemetry, T-07 PQC signaling, T-03 tabular grid rules) to form a unified, multi-layered architecture.
