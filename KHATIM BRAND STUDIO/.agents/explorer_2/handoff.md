## 2. Logic Chain

1. **From Observation 1 & 5 to Product-Brand Positioning Alignment:**
   - Because the category is defined as *Digital Trust Infrastructure* (D-001) but the initial MVP is a document sealing/signing application (05-POSITIONING-SCOPE.md), calling the MVP "infrastructure" without qualification creates the *MVP Reality Gap* (Red Team Finding 3).
   - Therefore, creative territories must articulate *Verifiable Digital Sealing* today while maintaining the architectural and symbolic capacity to stretch into *Digital Trust Infrastructure* tomorrow (D-009).

2. **From Observation 2, 3 & Red Team Review to Visual Territory Architecture:**
   - Because category competitors converge on blue shields, locks, and generic SaaS tropes (D-004), visual territories must draw from the 6 approved semiotic families (Imprint, Closure, Witness, Continuity, Convergence, Attribution) and balance the 7 visual tensions.
   - However, per Red Team Finding 13, a single mark must not be overburdened with communicating all 5 cryptographic layers simultaneously. The geometric mark must remain scalable down to 16px, delegating complex storytelling to typography, editorial layouts, and UI micro-interactions.

3. **From Observation 4 & Decision Log to Anti-Lock Boundaries:**
   - Because Codegic already markets an identical product suite under the "Khatim" name in Nice Classes 9, 38, and 42 (04-STRATEGIC-VALIDATION.md), and clearance is unresolved (D-005), any creative territory that relies strictly on the word "Khatim" or its specific letterforms is vulnerable to total invalidation if Route C (alternative naming) is triggered.
   - Therefore, all Phase 2 territory explorations must be conceptually autonomous, working equally well whether the name is *Khatim*, *Khatim Trust*, or a semantic alternative like *Tasdeeq* or *Sanad*.
   - Furthermore, all Phase 2 deliverables must strictly exclude finalized logo locks, hex color palettes, font licenses, or design tokens (D-005, P-001 through P-005).

4. **From Observation 6 to Objective Evaluation Engine:**
   - Applying the 8 weighted criteria (totalling 100%) provides an objective scoring mechanism to evaluate the 8 Phase 2 territories, ensuring subjective preference is subordinated to strategic differentiation (20%), strategic fit (20%), and commercial credibility (10%).
