# KHATIM — FIRST ANTIGRAVITY SESSION SCRIPT

## 0 — Open
Open the KHATIM Brand Studio project and confirm:
- `.agents/agents/`
- `.agents/skills/`
- `.agents/rules/`
- `.agents/workflows/`
- `research/KHATIM-BRANDING-RESEARCH/`
- `strategy/`

## 1 — Launch
Paste `strategy/MASTER-LAUNCH-PROMPT.md`.

Then send:

> Begin Phase 1 only. Ingest the research package and establish strategic control. Do not design yet.

## 2 — Evidence
Send:

> Build an evidence matrix. Classify all major claims as FACT, OBSERVATION, INTERPRETATION, HYPOTHESIS, or RECOMMENDATION. Do not invent missing evidence.

Expected:
`strategy/evidence/EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md`

## 3 — Competitive challenge
Send:

> Spawn the Research Analyst and Brand Strategist. Independently answer: “What does the digital trust/signature category already own visually and verbally, and what credible whitespace exists for Khatim?” Reconcile the findings. Do not design.

Expected:
`strategy/competitive/`

## 4 — Hypothesis test
Send:

> Test H1–H5 from the master prompt. Each agent must present counter-evidence before ranking the hypotheses.

Expected:
`strategy/HYPOTHESIS-TEST.md`

## 5 — Red team
Send:

> Activate the Brand Critic as an adversarial reviewer. Try to falsify the current strategic direction. Assume the strategy is wrong until defended by evidence.

Expected:
`strategy/RED-TEAM-REVIEW.md`

## 6 — Lock Phase 1
Send:

> Update the strategic control documents using the red-team findings. Clearly mark what is frozen, provisional, and open. Then stop. Do not start visual exploration.

## 7 — Human review
Review:
1. `strategy/02-STRATEGIC-CONTROL.md`
2. `strategy/03-CREATIVE-DIRECTION-DECISION.md`
3. `strategy/PROJECT-MEMORY.md`
4. `strategy/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`
5. `strategy/RED-TEAM-REVIEW.md`

Only after review run:
`/03-territory-exploration`

## Success criteria
Phase 1 is successful only when:
- the research is ingested
- evidence is traceable
- assumptions are labeled
- H1–H5 have been challenged
- category conventions are extracted
- naming risk is visible
- the brand idea is broader than “seal + cybersecurity”
- future expansion is incorporated
- visual clichés are explicit
- no final logo or identity has been prematurely selected
