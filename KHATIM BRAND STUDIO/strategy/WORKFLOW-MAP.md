## Parallelization

During `/03-territory-exploration`, the creative-director can fan out research-informed territory assignments to independent subagents.

During `/06-brand-system`, iconography, patterns, typography studies, and illustration studies may run in parallel after the visual grammar is frozen.

During `/07-applications`, website/UI, corporate documents, presentation templates, and social templates may run in parallel after the system is approved.

Never parallelize tasks that depend on a frozen decision that has not yet been approved.
