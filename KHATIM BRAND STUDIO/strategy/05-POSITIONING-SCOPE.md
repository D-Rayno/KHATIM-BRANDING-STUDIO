# 05 Positioning Scope: Reality vs. Ambition

## Objective
Address the "MVP Reality Gap" by defining the boundaries between what Khatim is today (product reality), what it will be tomorrow (near-term positioning), and what it aspires to be (long-term vision).

## 1. Current Product Reality (The MVP)
- **Scope:** The initial product release (MVP) will likely focus on core document signing and sealing workflows (a PDF signer equivalent).
- **Functionality:** Authenticating a user, capturing intent, applying a digital signature/seal to a document, and generating an audit trail.
- **The Gap:** A PDF signer is *not* "Digital Trust Infrastructure." Claiming infrastructure status with a workflow app risks severe credibility damage with enterprise IT/Security buyers (CISOs, CTOs).

## 2. Credible Near-Term Positioning
- **Positioning:** **Verifiable Digital Sealing** (or Advanced Digital Signatures).
- **Narrative:** Moving beyond "fast e-signatures" to "defensible digital seals." The focus is on the integrity, attributability, and finality of the specific document or transaction.
- **Audience Focus:** COOs, GCs, and compliance officers who need confidence that a signed document will hold up in court.
- **Stress-Test:** This positioning is credible for an MVP because it accurately describes the product's function while introducing a premium, evidence-based narrative (the "quiet authority" and "officiality" angles).

## 3. Long-Term Brand Ambition
- **Vision:** **Digital Trust Infrastructure**.
- **Scope:** A comprehensive platform encompassing identity verification, verifiable credentials, API-first trust services, PKI, and automated compliance ledgers (eIDAS 2.0 / NIST 2025 aligned).
- **Narrative:** Reusable trust infrastructure that developers and enterprises can plug into any workflow to guarantee identity, intent, and integrity.
- **Stress-Test:** This is an excellent North Star. However, it cannot be the *current* positioning until the product architecture matures to support APIs, credentials, and non-document trust services.

## 4. The Phased Brand Strategy
We must adopt a phased messaging architecture:
1. **Phase 1 (MVP Launch):** Lead with the *application* ("Khatim Sign/Seal") positioned around Verifiable Completion and Officiality.
2. **Phase 2 (Platform Expansion):** Introduce the *infrastructure* narrative as identity and API products launch, transitioning the brand from a "tool" to a "platform."
