# 14 Phase 3 Gate

## Phase 2.75 Completion Status

We are at the threshold of Phase 3 (Visual Identity Sprints). This gate defines the exact parameters under which design exploration may begin, acknowledging the critical business blockers that remain unresolved.

---

### 1. What is SAFE to explore now
- **The Concept of the "Matrix and Impression":** Exploring the geometric physics of a positive die and a negative deboss.
- **The Tripartite Layout:** Exploring grids and visual structures that balance three entities (A, B, Witness).
- **The Subterranean Grid:** Building proportion systems based on the 45° rhombic *nuqta*.
- **The UI Behavior:** Exploring how evidentiary brackets `[ ... ]` and monospace tables look in a product interface.
- **Typography and Color Moods:** Broad explorations of "institutional authority" and "tactile permanence."

### 2. What remains PROVISIONAL
- **The "Sovereign Witness" Narrative:** This is an *internal strategic concept only*. It is not approved customer-facing language until buyer validation confirms it doesn't cause market friction.
- **The Logomark:** Any symbols generated are sketches testing the Semiotic Center, not final candidates.

### 3. What requires ACTUAL Legal/Business validation (The Blockers)
- **The Khatim Name:** A formal legal trademark search and opinion regarding the Codegic collision must be executed. **No final wordmark can be locked until this is resolved.**
- **The MVP Scope:** Engineering must confirm that V1 can technically produce the "tamper-evident audit trails" required by the brand narrative.
- **Buyer Acceptance:** The Buyer Validation Protocol (Strategy/12) must be executed with real CISOs and COOs.

### 4. What Phase 3 MUST prove
Phase 3 is an exploratory sprint. It must prove that:
1. The concept of "The Witness / The Deboss" can be visualized elegantly in digital software without looking like legacy skeuomorphism.
2. The visual system does not rely on the name "Khatim" to carry its structural meaning.
3. The brand can look "secure and authoritative" without using a single shield, lock, or blue gradient.

> **GATE DECISION:** Proceed to Phase 3 (Exploration Only). Do not finalize any assets.
