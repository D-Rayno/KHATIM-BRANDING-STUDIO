## Decision rules

1. Strategy outranks aesthetics.
2. Distinctiveness outranks trendiness.
3. System coherence outranks isolated visual novelty.
4. Arabic and Latin identity must be designed as one system.
5. The brand must scale beyond signing into broader digital trust infrastructure.
6. Generic cybersecurity, generic fintech, and generic SaaS conventions require explicit justification.
7. Every irreversible design decision needs an evidence trail.

## Evidence tags

Use these labels in working documents:

- FACT — directly supported by a cited source or supplied research.
- OBSERVATION — directly visible in a competitor/design sample.
- INTERPRETATION — reasoned reading of evidence.
- HYPOTHESIS — plausible but unverified.
- RECOMMENDATION — strategic choice proposed for Khatim.
