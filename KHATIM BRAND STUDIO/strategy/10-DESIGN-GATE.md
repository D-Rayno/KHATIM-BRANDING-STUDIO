# 10 Design Gate

## Status: BLOCKED 🛑

Phase 2.5 (Creative Convergence) is complete. The brand has been successfully narrowed to a single, highly defensible Core Brand Idea: **The Sovereign Witness**, supported by the visual semiotics of **The Matrix and the Impression**.

However, the Brand Critic's adversarial review has mandated a hard block on proceeding to visual identity sprints (Phase 3) until critical business risks are resolved.

## Brand Critic Challenge & Verdict
> "The design team will spend months failing to reconcile opposing visual languages if they attempt a tripartite model. We have successfully dismantled that bloat. The brand is now sharp. **BUT**, the foundation is critically endangered by the open Codegic trademark collision. Furthermore, designing an 'institutional trust infrastructure' identity for an MVP that might just be a PDF signer is a recipe for a massive reality gap."

## Blockers for Phase 3 (Visual Identity Sprints)

1. **🔴 Legal Name Clearance (Codegic Collision):**
   - **Requirement:** Formal legal opinion on the viability of the name "Khatim" or "Khatim Trust" in Nice Classes 9, 38, 42. 
   - **Why:** While the "Witness" strategy survives a name change, the logo mark's exact geometry and typography cannot be designed until the name is confirmed or Route C (fallback name) is invoked.

2. **🔴 MVP Product Scope Definition:**
   - **Requirement:** Technical confirmation of what the V1 product actually does.
   - **Why:** The UI behavior relies on forensic evidence and hash telemetry. If the MVP cannot support this technically, the brand will launch as vaporware.

3. **🔴 Buyer Validation:**
   - **Requirement:** Qualitative interviews with target CISOs and COOs.
   - **Why:** To ensure the "Sovereign Witness" posture is perceived as premium authority and not unnecessary bureaucratic friction.

## Final Design Brief Constraints (When Unblocked)
When Phase 3 begins, the designers MUST adhere to:
- **Lead Idea:** The Sovereign Witness (Tactile Archival Deboss)
- **Grid:** 45° Rhombic Nuqta Grid
- **UI Element:** Evidentiary Brackets `[ ... ]`
- **Exclusions:** NO literal shields, locks, keys, checkmarks. NO generic arabesque patterns. NO blue cybersecurity gradients. NO equal weighting of T-02, T-04, and T-06.
