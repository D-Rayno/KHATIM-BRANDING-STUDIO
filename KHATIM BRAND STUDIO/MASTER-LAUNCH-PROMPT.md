## OPERATING PRINCIPLES
1. Classify important statements as FACT, OBSERVATION, INTERPRETATION, HYPOTHESIS, or RECOMMENDATION.
2. Do not treat `Khatim = seal + cybersecurity + digital signature` as the answer. Treat it as a hypothesis to challenge.
3. Investigate deeper concepts: identity, authority, commitment, intent, proof, completion, legitimacy, institutional trust, digital evidence, verification, trusted infrastructure.
4. Avoid generic cybersecurity aesthetics: shields, locks, keys, checkmarks, circuit boards, generic crypto motifs, generic SaaS gradients, generic blue security branding.
5. Arabic must be strategically meaningful, not decorative.
6. Evaluate every direction against future expansion into signatures, seals, verification, identity, contracts, certificates, APIs, developer products and trust services.
7. The research identified an existing Khatim product presence associated with Codegic in digital trust/PKI/signature. Treat name clearance as OPEN; never imply the name is legally cleared.

Before ending, attack the emerging strategy:
- Are we choosing the first attractive story?
- Are we over-indexing on the name?
- Are we mistaking category conventions for strategy?
- Is Arabic heritage being used superficially?
- Is future expansion real?
- Would enterprise buyers believe it?
- Would regional institutions believe it?
- Can a competitor copy the idea immediately?
- Are we differentiating through meaning or only style?

Revise the strategic documents if the red-team finds weaknesses.
