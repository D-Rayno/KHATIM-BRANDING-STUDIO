# Logo Opportunities

Explore marks that can operate at favicon size, app icon size, PDF validation panels, enterprise stationery and physical seals.

Potential structural principles:
- one memorable gesture
- strong negative space
- bilingual resilience
- geometric logic that can animate
- ability to become a verification stamp or status marker
- works in one color before any palette is added

Avoid creating a logo that literally depicts a stamp, lock or signature unless the form is unusually ownable.
