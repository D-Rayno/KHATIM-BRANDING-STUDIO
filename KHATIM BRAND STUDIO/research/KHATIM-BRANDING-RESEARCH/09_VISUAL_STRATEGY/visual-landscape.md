# Visual Landscape

Competitors span two dominant visual worlds:

1. **Clean SaaS/document:** white space, product UI, approachable sans-serif, simple trust cues.
2. **Security/infrastructure:** dark backgrounds, purple/blue, high-contrast geometry, shields, hexagons, networks, technical imagery.

The opportunity is not to become “more distinctive” through decoration. It is to build a **specific symbolic grammar** around mark, proof, closure, identity and continuity.
