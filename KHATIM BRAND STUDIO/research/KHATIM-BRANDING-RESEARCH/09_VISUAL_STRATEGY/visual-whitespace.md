# Visual Whitespace

### Opportunity 1 — Editorial authority
A more sophisticated information-design language rather than cyber effects.

### Opportunity 2 — Imprint logic
Use repeated marks, depth, pressure, negative space or registration concepts to suggest evidence.

### Opportunity 3 — Closure
Visual transitions can move from open/uncertain to closed/verified.

### Opportunity 4 — Bilingual intelligence
Arabic and Latin can coexist as a deliberate system rather than an afterthought.
