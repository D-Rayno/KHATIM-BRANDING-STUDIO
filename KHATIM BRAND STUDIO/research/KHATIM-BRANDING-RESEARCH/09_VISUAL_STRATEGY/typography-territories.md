# Typography Territories

1. **Precision grotesk:** high clarity, enterprise, product-led.
2. **Humanist sans:** approachable authority.
3. **Geometric sans:** modernity and system thinking; risk of category sameness.
4. **Editorial grotesk + Arabic companion:** more premium/institutional.
5. **Custom bilingual wordmark:** strongest differentiation if the name is kept.

Selection must be tested across Arabic, French and English interfaces, numeric data, legal documents and dense technical content.
