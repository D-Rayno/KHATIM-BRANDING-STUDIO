# Arabic–Latin Identity

The bilingual identity should not treat Arabic as a translation layer. It should be a first-class design system.

### Explore
- shared stroke logic between Arabic and Latin
- matched optical weight rather than literal geometric matching
- consistent rhythm and spacing
- Arabic as a source for bespoke brand geometry
- wordmark lockups that remain readable at small sizes

### Avoid
- decorative calligraphy that disappears in product UI
- forcing Latin letters into Arabic shapes without legibility testing
- a different visual personality for each language.
