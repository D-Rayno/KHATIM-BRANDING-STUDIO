# Visual Territories

1. **The Modern Imprint** — abstract mark based on pressure, registration and evidence.
2. **The Closed Loop** — completion and finality, but not a literal seal.
3. **The Witness Mark** — a discrete sign of verified origin.
4. **The Convergence** — identities/intent/data meeting.
5. **The Digital Signet** — contemporary interpretation of signet logic.
6. **The Provenance Line** — traceability and continuity.
7. **The Trust Ledger** — structured, authoritative visual system without blockchain cliché.

These are creative territories, not logo concepts.
