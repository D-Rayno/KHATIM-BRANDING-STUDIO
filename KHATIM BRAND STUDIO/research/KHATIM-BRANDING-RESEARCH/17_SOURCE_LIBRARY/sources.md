# Khatim — Source Library

Research cut-off: **26 August 2026**.

The package prioritizes first-party, government, regulator, standards and museum/academic sources. Branding-firm articles are used only for visual-landscape observations and are explicitly treated as secondary evidence.

## Source discipline

- **Fact:** directly supported by a source.
- **Observation:** a pattern observed across source material.
- **Interpretation:** strategic reading of facts/observations.
- **Hypothesis:** plausible but still requiring validation through customer research, legal review or design testing.
- **Recommendation:** proposed action based on the synthesis.

See [`sources.csv`](sources.csv) for the structured database.
