# Semiotic Opportunities

## Highest-potential semantic families
1. **Imprint / mark** — evidence of origin.
2. **Closure** — a state becoming final.
3. **Witness** — a trusted third party that makes an action defensible.
4. **Continuity** — evidence that survives time.
5. **Convergence** — parties/intent/data meeting in a trusted state.
6. **Attribution** — the mark ties an action to identity.

The brand should avoid collapsing “trust” into generic protection symbolism.
