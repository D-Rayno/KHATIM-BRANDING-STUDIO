# Competitor Landscape

| Cluster | Players | Core mental model |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement workflow | DocuSign, Adobe Acrobat Sign, Dropbox Sign, PandaDoc, SignNow | “Get agreements signed and managed” |
| Digital trust/security | OneSpan, Entrust, DigiCert, GlobalSign | “High assurance, PKI, identity, security” |
| European trust | Yousign, Universign, Docaposte, Skribble | “Compliant, sovereign, legally trusted e-signature” |
| Regional trust | Ethaq Plus, emdha, Signit, SDAIA services, Barid eSign | “Locally trusted, regulated digital transactions” |
| Infrastructure | SPI-S, Codegic Khatim products | “Deploy the trust/signing layer inside enterprise systems” |

Khatim could occupy the gap between workflow platform and trust infrastructure: a **trust platform with a modern product experience**.
