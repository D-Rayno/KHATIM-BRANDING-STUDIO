# Competitor Visual Analysis

## Common codes
- blue / navy as primary trust color
- purple accents in security/identity
- geometric sans-serif wordmarks
- abstract network or connection graphics
- checks, shields and document visuals
- gradients and luminous digital effects in security-led brands
- product UI as the primary visual proof

## Strategic read
Docusign’s 2024 identity is especially instructive: a market leader consciously moved away from signature-only equity toward a broader platform story. [S21][S22]

## Opportunity
Khatim can use a more **editorial, institutional and symbolic visual language**, grounded in the semantics of imprint/closure/proof rather than cyber ornament.
