# Competitor Strengths & Weaknesses

| Cluster | Strength | Weakness / opening |
|---|---|---|
| DocuSign/Adobe | Massive trust, distribution, recognizable workflows | Signature/document associations can constrain new-category narratives |
| Dropbox/PandaDoc | Strong ease and workflow UX | Less distinctive as “trust infrastructure” brands |
| OneSpan/Entrust | Deep technical/regulatory credibility | Can feel infrastructure-heavy and technical |
| European TSPs | Legal/sovreign credibility | Often more compliance-led than emotionally distinctive |
| Regional providers | Local trust and regulatory fit | Fragmented brand equity; scale varies |
| Codegic Khatim | Strong technical Khatim naming equity in PKI | Creates a direct collision for a new Khatim brand |
