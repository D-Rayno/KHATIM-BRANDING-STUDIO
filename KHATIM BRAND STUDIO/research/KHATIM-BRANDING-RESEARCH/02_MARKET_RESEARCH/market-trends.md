# Market Trends

## 1. Trust services are broadening
EU policy now treats signatures, seals, timestamps, archiving, registered delivery, attestations and electronic ledgers as parts of a wider trust framework. [S01][S02]

## 2. Identity is joining the signing flow
Docusign, OneSpan, Entrust and regional providers increasingly combine signing with identity verification/authentication. [S19][S29][S31][S15][S16]

## 3. Wallets change the distribution model
EU Digital Identity Wallet work introduces reusable identity and credential primitives that can sit before, during or after a transaction. [S03][S04]

## 4. Standards and interoperability matter more
ETSI and the European Commission are explicitly working on interfaces between wallets, trust services, relying parties and attestations. [S05][S06]

## 5. “Trust” is becoming observable
Trust centers, audit trails, proof files, certification lists, timestamps and evidence packages are increasingly productized. [S20][S24][S26]

## 6. Sovereignty is a commercial story
Regional providers increasingly emphasize local licensing, regulatory alignment, data residency or government backing. [S13][S16][S37]
