# Market Threats

- Commodity e-signature pricing.
- Incumbents bundling signature into larger document suites.
- Trust infrastructure becoming invisible behind operating systems or wallets.
- Regulation fragmenting by country.
- Procurement favouring established global brands.
- “Trust platform” becoming generic language without a distinctive proof point.
- New cryptographic and identity standards creating implementation costs.

Khatim therefore needs both a **strong category story** and **proof-backed credibility**.
