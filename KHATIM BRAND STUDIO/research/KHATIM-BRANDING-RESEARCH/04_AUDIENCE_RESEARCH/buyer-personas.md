# Buyer Personas

## The Risk Owner
CISO/compliance/legal. Wants defensibility, evidence, policy control and predictable regulatory fit.

## The Transformation Owner
CIO/operations/HR/procurement. Wants faster workflows and fewer manual handoffs without introducing new risk.

## The Trust Architect
PKI/security/IT architect. Wants standards, HSM compatibility, deployment control, interoperability and long-term validation.

## The Platform Builder
Developer/product lead. Wants APIs, clear abstractions and trust capabilities that can disappear into the product experience.

## The Signer
Employee/customer/citizen. Wants confidence, clarity and minimal friction; they do not want to learn PKI to complete a transaction.
