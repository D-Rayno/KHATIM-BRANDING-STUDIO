# Audience Segmentation

| Segment | Primary job | Trust threshold | What wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Government | Issue/approve/verify official records | Very high | Sovereignty, legal status, resilience |
| Banking/financial | Execute and evidence high-value actions | Very high | Identity assurance, auditability, uptime |
| Legal/compliance | Defensible evidence | Very high | Legal clarity, proof, preservation |
| Enterprise operations | Reduce friction | High | Workflow UX + governance |
| HR/procurement | Onboard / contract quickly | High | Ease + control + audit trail |
| Developers | Embed trust into software | High | APIs, standards, reliability |
| SME/professional | Get things signed simply | Medium-high | Ease, affordability, trust cues |
