# Trust Drivers

## Hard signals
Licensing, qualification, standards, certifications, trusted lists, HSM-backed keys, audit logs, timestamps, evidence preservation, identity verification, data residency and uptime.

## Soft signals
Clarity, restraint, institutional maturity, consistency, transparent language, strong customer proof and a calm interface.

**Brand principle:** Khatim should turn hard trust signals into understandable product experiences, not replace them with visual “trust theatre.”
