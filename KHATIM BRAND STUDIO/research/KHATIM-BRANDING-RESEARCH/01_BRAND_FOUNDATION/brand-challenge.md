# Brand Challenge

Khatim must solve four problems simultaneously:

1. **Category sameness:** most trust/security products already look credible.
2. **Complexity:** cryptography, PKI and regulation are difficult to explain.
3. **Trust burden:** buyers need proof, not marketing theatrics.
4. **Name risk:** an exact same-category Khatim product family already exists in the market.

The branding challenge is therefore not “make security attractive”; it is to create a **recognizable mental category and proof-bearing brand system** while validating whether the name can be safely owned.
