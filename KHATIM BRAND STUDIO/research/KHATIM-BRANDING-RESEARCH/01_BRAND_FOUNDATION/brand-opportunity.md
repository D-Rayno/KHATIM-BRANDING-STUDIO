# Brand Opportunity

The deepest opportunity is to move from the object being signed to the **state created by trusted action**.

A contract before signing is intention. After signing, sealing and verification, it becomes evidence. Khatim can represent that transformation.

Potential strategic language:
- proof
- finality
- validity
- provenance
- attributable intent
- official state
- trusted completion

These are more future-proof than “signature,” because they can extend to identity, attestations, seals, verification and trust APIs.
