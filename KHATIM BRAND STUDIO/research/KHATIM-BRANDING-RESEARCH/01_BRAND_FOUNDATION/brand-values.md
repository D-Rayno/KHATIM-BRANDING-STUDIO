# Brand Values

1. **Proof over promise** — credibility must be evidenced.
2. **Precision** — trust depends on exactness.
3. **Clarity** — complexity should be translated, not hidden behind jargon.
4. **Respect** — for users, law, identity and cultural context.
5. **Interoperability** — trust should work across systems and borders.
6. **Continuity** — signed/sealed evidence should remain verifiable over time.
