# Strategic Recommendation

## Recommended direction
1. **Immediately run legal/trademark/domain clearance.** The current same-category Khatim use is too material to ignore.
2. If the name is viable, pursue a **Digital Trust / Trusted Completion** positioning rather than “e-signature software.”
3. Build the identity around **imprint + proof + completion**, not shield + lock + signature.
4. Make Arabic heritage structural and semantic, not ornamental.
5. Design the brand architecture for signatures, seals, verification, identity, evidence and APIs.
6. Validate the strategic idea with enterprise/legal/technical buyers before final logo development.

## Core hypothesis
**Khatim can become the brand associated with the moment a digital action becomes official, provable and trustworthy.**

This is more differentiated and future-proof than “seal + cybersecurity + digital signature.”
