# International Name Analysis

## Strengths
- 6 Latin characters; compact wordmark.
- Distinctive compared with generic compounds such as SecureSign, TrustSign or SignHub.
- Easy to keep visually minimal.

## Challenges
- Pronunciation may vary between English/French speakers.
- Meaning is not self-evident outside Arabic-speaking contexts.
- Search engines may surface unrelated/name-collision results.
- “Khatim” already has technology-category usage.

The international story should therefore be built around a clear descriptor and a consistent pronunciation guide, after legal clearance.
