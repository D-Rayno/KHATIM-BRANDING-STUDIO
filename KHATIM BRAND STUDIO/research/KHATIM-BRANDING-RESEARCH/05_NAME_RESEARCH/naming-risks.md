# Naming Risks

## Critical — same-category collision
Codegic currently markets KhatimDoc, Khatim Sign Server, Khatim PKI Server, Khatim Timestamp Server and Khatim Verification Server. [S38][S39][S40][S41]

## High — trademark clearance
A trademark attorney should search the exact mark plus Arabic script and relevant Nice classes in intended launch jurisdictions. Web presence is evidence of use, not a legal opinion.

## High — search/SEO confusion
An overlapping technology family could make it difficult for Khatim to dominate branded search.

## Medium — international semantics
The word’s meaning requires explanation outside Arabic-speaking audiences.

## Medium — pronunciation
English/French speakers may vary on “KHA-tim” vs. “kha-TEEM” depending on language.

## Decision gate
**Do not finalize naming/identity until clearance determines whether coexistence is commercially and legally realistic.**
