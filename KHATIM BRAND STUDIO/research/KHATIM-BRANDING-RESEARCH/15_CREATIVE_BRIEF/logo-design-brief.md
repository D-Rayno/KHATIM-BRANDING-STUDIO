# Logo Design Brief

## Objective
Create an identity mark capable of representing digital trust beyond signing.

## Must communicate
Authority, proof, precision, identity, completion.

## Explore
imprint, closure, witness mark, signet logic, convergence, provenance.

## Functional requirements
Works in one color, favicon, app icon, PDF verification context, enterprise presentations, physical seal, Arabic/Latin lockups.

## Avoid
Literal lock, shield, keyhole, checkmark, pen stroke, generic circular seal.

## Important
Do not finalize a symbol until the name itself is legally/commercially cleared.
