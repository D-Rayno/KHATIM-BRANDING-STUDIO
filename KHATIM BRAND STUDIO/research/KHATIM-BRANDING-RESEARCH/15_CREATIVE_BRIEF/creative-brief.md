# Creative Brief

## Brand
Khatim (خاتم), subject to naming clearance.

## Business
Digital trust platform spanning signature, seal, identity, verification and cryptographic infrastructure.

## Audience
Enterprise, regulated organizations, government, legal/compliance, IT/security, developers and signers.

## Problem
The category is crowded and visually generic. The brand must earn trust without looking like every cybersecurity/e-signature company.

## Single-minded idea
**Make digital actions count.**

## Emotional response
“I know this is real, attributable and final.”

## Desired character
Calm authority, intelligent precision, modern trust, cultural depth.

## Creative task
Translate the semantics of imprint, proof, closure and identity into an ownable bilingual identity system.

## Avoid
Literal shield/lock/key/scribble, cyber clichés, generic blue gradients, decorative Arabic motifs.

## Success
A buyer should recognize Khatim as a serious trust company while perceiving it as distinct from standard e-signature and cybersecurity vendors.
