# Strategic Tensions

### Human ↔ machine
The brand must speak to signers and cryptographic architects.

### Heritage ↔ future
Use history as semantic fuel, not a nostalgic aesthetic.

### Authority ↔ approachability
Trust requires seriousness; UX requires ease.

### Specific ↔ expandable
The first product may be signing, but the brand should not be trapped there.

### Visible ↔ invisible
Users need clarity on trust, while the infrastructure should become effortless.
