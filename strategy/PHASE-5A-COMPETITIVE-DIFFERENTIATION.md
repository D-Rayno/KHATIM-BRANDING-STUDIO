# PHASE 5A — Competitive Differentiation Test

> **Date:** 2026-08-28
> **Method:** Adversarial review of 3 complete identity directions
> **Standard:** 6 kill questions per direction

---

## Direction 1: Mineral Authority
- **Mark:** F1 (unchanged)
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk + Tajawal
- **Color:** Deep Mineral (slate `#1E293B`, mineral teal `#0F766E`, pale sage `#E2E8D8`)
- **Personality:** Calm, grounded, permanent, natural authority

### Kill Questions

**Q1: If the logo were removed, would this still look like a generic cybersecurity company?**
> The mineral palette (slate + sage + deep teal) is not used by any major cybersecurity or trust company. The typography (Space Grotesk) has character but is not overused in this category. **NO — the color alone differentiates.**

**Q2: If the color were removed, would the identity still be distinctive?**
> F1's asymmetric K∩خ silhouette is distinctive regardless of color. The typography pairing is specific. **YES — the mark and type carry distinction.**

**Q3: If the Arabic meaning were not explained, would the system still feel intentional?**
> The bilingual lockup is structurally balanced. The symbol acts as a pivot. A non-Arabic viewer sees a bold monogram + geometric wordmark. **YES — the system feels authored.**

**Q4: Could DocuSign copy the system in six months?**
> DocuSign's current palette is cobalt + poppy + ecru. Moving to mineral slate + sage would be a complete rebrand. The F1 mark is not copyable without looking like a direct clone. **NO — would require abandoning their 2024 rebrand.**

**Q5: Could a startup generate something similar with an AI logo generator?**
> AI generators produce symmetric stars, gradient blobs, and generic geometric marks. F1's asymmetric arms + I'jam dot position are too specific. The mineral palette is not in AI default outputs. **NO.**

**Q6: Does the identity have a recognizable proprietary grammar?**
> Yes: 45° lattice construction, I'jam dot as verification state, stem-lattice patterns, mineral color register. The grammar is consistent and ownable.

### Direction 1 Verdict: **PASS** (strong differentiation)

## Direction 2: Warm Sovereign
- **Mark:** F1 (unchanged)
- **Typography:** IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Arabic
- **Color:** Warm Institutional (deep umber `#3D2B1F`, warm cream `#F5F0E8`, terracotta `#C4703A`)

### Kill Questions

**Q1: If the logo were removed, would this still look like a generic cybersecurity company?**
> The warm umber + terracotta palette is genuinely unusual in trust tech. Most competitors are blue, purple, or black. **NO.**

**Q2: If the color were removed, would the identity still be distinctive?**
> F1 + IBM Plex is a strong combination. IBM Plex has technical authority. **YES.**

**Q3: If the Arabic meaning were not explained, would the system still feel intentional?**
> IBM Plex Arabic is designed as a companion to the Latin. The integration is seamless. **YES.**

**Q4: Could DocuSign copy the system in six months?**
> Warm umber is far from DocuSign's cobalt. IBM Plex is widely available but the combination with F1 is specific. **NO.**

**Q5: Could a startup generate something similar with an AI logo generator?**
> AI does not default to umber + terracotta. F1's geometry is too specific. **NO.**

**Q6: Does the identity have a recognizable proprietary grammar?**
> Yes, though slightly less distinctive than Direction 1 because IBM Plex is more common.

### Direction 2 Verdict: **PASS** (good differentiation, slightly warmer tone)
