## Priority 1 — Could Change Everything

> [!CAUTION]
> These questions have the power to invalidate current strategy. They must be resolved before committing to irreversible brand decisions.

### 1. Can the name "Khatim" be legally cleared?

**Why it matters:** Codegic markets an exact same-category product family under the name "Khatim." If trademark clearance fails in target jurisdictions (Nice Classes 9, 38, 42), Routes C or D require a fundamentally different brand approach — new name, new visual identity, new verbal system.

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk level** | 🔴 Existential |
| **Blocking** | All final visual identity work |
| **Owner** | Legal / Founder |
| **Status** | In Progress (Phase 1.5 Validation) |

---

### 2. What is the actual MVP product scope?

**Why it matters:** If Khatim launches as a lightweight PDF signer, claiming "Digital Trust Infrastructure" risks credibility. The product roadmap directly constrains the brand claim. A signing-only launch requires a different narrative strategy than a multi-service trust platform launch.

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk level** | 🔴 Strategic |
| **Blocking** | Positioning credibility, messaging hierarchy |
| **Owner** | Product / Founder |
| **Status** | In Progress (Phase 1.5 Validation) |

### 3. Do enterprise buyers actually value "officiality" and "defensible proof" over workflow speed?

**Why it matters:** The positioning hypothesis — that buyers want evidence-grade trust, not just convenience — is untested with real buyers. If speed-to-sign is the dominant purchase driver, the entire differentiation strategy shifts.

## Priority 2 — Could Significantly Affect Direction

> [!WARNING]
> These questions won't invalidate the strategy but could force significant adjustments to naming, messaging, or market approach.

### 4. How will non-Arabic speakers perceive and pronounce "Khatim"?

**Why it matters:** The "Kh" phoneme (خ) is challenging in many Western languages. Pronunciation variance (KHA-tim vs. kha-TEEM vs. KAT-im) could fragment brand recognition and create friction in sales conversations.

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk level** | 🟡 High |
| **Affects** | Naming, verbal identity, international sales |
| **Status** | Not started |

---

### 5. Does the Arabic colloquial meaning (خاتم = ring/jewelry) create confusion in Arabic-speaking markets?

**Why it matters:** The strategic meaning (seal/completion) differs from the everyday meaning most Arabic speakers associate with the word. This gap could dilute the intended positioning in MENA markets — the brand's cultural home base.

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk level** | 🟡 High |
| **Affects** | MENA market positioning, naming |
| **Status** | Not started |
