## 2. The Verdict

### Primary: F1 — The Witnessed Letter
**Status:** APPROVED for canonical development.

**Score:** 8.35/10 (3.3-point margin over nearest reserve)

**Why it passes:**
- The protecting idea (I'jam dot as identity-decider) is **visible in the resting silhouette**, uncaptioned.
- Not a container, not a seal, not a check, not a shield.
- Structurally Arabic without being decorative.
- Internationally credible without requiring Arabic knowledge.
- 16px-safe, monochrome-native, reverse-native.
- High system potential (patterns, motion, verification UI).

**Conditions:**
1. **Canonical geometry lock:** The dot must remain at the I'jam register (centered above stem, never at arm terminus). Any drift toward check/tick position voids the mark.
2. **Speed to register:** The exact geometric specification must be filed for trademark protection. The premise is not proprietary; the execution is.
3. **Typography gate:** Final wordmark typography is Phase 5-gated. All current wordmarks are placeholders.
