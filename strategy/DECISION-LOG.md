## Decision Details

### D-001: Category Definition — Digital Trust Infrastructure
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Decision:** Khatim operates in the Digital Trust Infrastructure category, not the e-signature tool category.
- **Evidence:**
  - eIDAS 2.0 expands trust services beyond signatures to wallets, attestations, archiving, ledgers [S01][S02][S05][S08]
  - DocuSign rebranded to IAM in 2024, validating category expansion [S21][S22]
  - NIST 2025 integrates proofing, authentication, and federation into unified framework
  - Market bifurcation between lightweight SaaS and heavy PKI creates whitespace for infrastructure-grade UX
- **Alternatives Considered:**
  - "E-signature platform" — rejected as too narrow and commoditized
  - "Digital agreement platform" — rejected as DocuSign territory
  - "Cybersecurity platform" — rejected as wrong adjacency
- **Implications:** All messaging, visual identity, and product positioning must support infrastructure-level claims. MVP must demonstrate trust infrastructure capabilities, not just PDF signing.
- **Reversibility:** IRREVERSIBLE — downstream positioning, competitive framing, and creative territories depend on this.

---

### D-002: Brand Essence — "Make digital actions count"
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Decision:** Working internal brand essence, NOT a public tagline.
- **Evidence:**
  - Core customer problem = digital actions lack defensible proof of attributability, integrity, and finality
  - "Count" carries dual meaning: "matter" (significance) + "be counted upon" (reliability)
  - Distinguishes from competitor messaging focused on speed ("get it done") or security ("lock it down")
- **Alternatives Considered:**
  - "Trust made digital" — too generic
  - "The last word in digital trust" — too aggressive
  - "Where digital becomes official" — strong but less scalable
- **Implications:** Every feature and design decision should be tested against this essence.
- **Reversibility:** REVERSIBLE — can be refined without invalidating other decisions.

---

### D-003: Cultural Strategy — Arabic Heritage as Structural Meaning
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Decision:** Arabic heritage provides conceptual structure (identity + evidence + finality), not decoration.
- **Evidence:**
  - Root ختم (kh-t-m) = stamp/seal + complete/finish + reach the end [S42][S43]
  - Historical Islamic seals = identity/provenance evidence systems [S44][S45][S46]
  - Semantic distinction khatam/khatm maps directly to product function
- **Guardrails:**
  - No generic arabesque patterns
  - Arabic typography as first-class system, not translation
  - Cultural depth must attract international buyers, not alienate them
- **Reversibility:** IRREVERSIBLE — the name itself is the cultural anchor.

---

### D-004: Visual Direction — Reject Generic Cybersecurity Aesthetics
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Decision:** No shields, locks, keys, checkmarks, pen strokes, circuit boards, dark hacker aesthetic, or neon gradients.
- **Evidence:**
  - Competitive visual audit: 15 competitors converge on shields/locks/blue [S19][S23][S26]
  - Category visual homogeneity creates massive differentiation opportunity
  - "Loud security" contradicts the "quiet authority" emotional positioning
- **Reversibility:** IRREVERSIBLE — foundational design principle.

---

### D-005: Naming Gate — Legal Clearance Required Before Final Design
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Decision:** No final visual identity decisions until "Khatim" name is legally cleared.
- **Evidence:**
  - Codegic markets KhatimDoc, Khatim Sign Server, Khatim PKI Server, Khatim Timestamp Server, Khatim Verification Server [S38-S41]
  - Same Nice Classes (9, 38, 42) = direct collision risk
  - No clearance documentation obtained to date
- **Contingency Routes:**
  - A: Keep Khatim (clearance successful)
  - B: Khatim + descriptor (e.g., Khatim Trust)
  - C: New name from same semantic field
  - D: Corporate/product brand separation
- **Reversibility:** IRREVERSIBLE — all downstream visual work is contingent.
