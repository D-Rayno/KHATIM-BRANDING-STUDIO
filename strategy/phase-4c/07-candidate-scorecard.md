## Candidate F1 — The Witnessed Letter (Cross-Glyph K ∩ خ + I'jam dot)

### Blind read (no explanation shown)
- "Category?" → Abstract letterform / monogram. Not a seal, not a shield, not a check.
- "What do you remember?" → Bold angular K-like shape with a single dot above the stem. The dot is the only round/soft element in an otherwise sharp form.
- "What makes it different?" → The dot is NOT where a check/tick goes. It sits above the stem like a crown, not at the end of a stroke like a verification mark. The arms are asymmetric (upper longer, steeper; lower shorter, shallower) — not a generic star or asterisk.
- "What does it resemble?" → DigiCert (D+check) is the nearest genus, but F1's dot position is structurally different (graphemic crown vs. verification tail). Alkhafrah (K+خ fusion) is the same premise but different execution and category.

### Scores

| Criterion | Score | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Distinctiveness | 8/10 | Cross-glyph K∩خ is not common in trust category. The specific arm asymmetry + dot-above-stem creates a signature silhouette. Loses 2 points because "letter + dot" is a known genus (DigiCert, Alkhafrah). |
| Memorability | 8/10 | The dot-above-stem is the mnemonic hook. Two-second recall: "angular K with a crown dot." The asymmetry of the arms aids memory (not a symmetric star). |
| Silhouette | 8/10 | Solid mass, clear stem, two diagonals, one dot. At 16px the stem and arms survive; dot is ~2px and visible. Loses 2 points because extreme small sizes (favicon) may lose the dot — but the stem+arm K-silhouette still reads. |
| Strategic fit | 9/10 | Directly expresses "The Impression" + "Sovereign Witness" — the dot is the witness point that decides identity. The stem is the shared bilateral structure. The arms are the two parties converging. |
| Conceptual compression | 9/10 | One glyph = K (Latin) + خ (Arabic) + I'jam dot (verification state) + witness point (sovereign third party). Four meanings in one silhouette. |
| Cultural depth | 9/10 | The dot is structurally Arabic (I'jam that turns ح→خ), not decorative. A deep Arabic-typography observer recognizes the graphemic logic. Not tokenism — it IS the first letter of the word. |
| International viability | 8/10 | Reads as a bold abstract letter/monogram to non-Arabic viewers. The dot adds character without requiring explanation. The "K" reading is immediate. Loses 2 points because the Arabic reading requires knowledge, but the mark does not depend on it. |
| Small-size performance | 7/10 | 16px: stem (4px wide) + arms (diagonal mass) + dot (~2px) all visible. Favicon (16×16) the dot may become a single pixel but the K-silhouette remains distinctive. Reverse-native, monochrome. |
| System potential | 9/10 | Stem/arm/dot geometry seeds patterns, the dot drives verification-state UI (dot present = attested), motion verb is "witnessing reveal" (dot seats from above), wordmark integration is natural (K stem extends into wordmark). |

**Weighted total: 8.35/10**
