## Bottom line

**Build ≥3 symbol-family members around: cross-glyph K ∩ خ, where one structural, positionally-loaded nuqta is simultaneously the Witness's third point, the convergence knot, the closure cusp, and the I'jam operator that toggles خ/unwitnessed ح.** The dot must *decide the glyph's identity*, never decorate it. Motion = "irreversible witness reveal." All resting silhouettes must pass: remove the dot → the mark collapses into the wrong letter and loses its third point.
