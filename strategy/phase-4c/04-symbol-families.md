## Convergence note (why one family leads)

Agents **1 (Creative Director), 3 (Arabic structural), 4 (Semiotics), 6 (Competitive Red Team)** independently converged on the same structural principle — **the I'jam / positional dot** — as the only mechanism that is simultaneously:
- structurally Arabic (the dot that turns ح into خ, and خ starts the word خاتم),
- carries the verification-state product behavior (dot present = witnessed; absent = unwitnessed),
- copy-resistant (meaning is **positional**, not "primitive + boolean" recomposable),
- the "witness's third point / closure cusp / convergence knot" in one.

**Family 1** is developed around that principle as a **cross-glyph K ∩ خ composite** (per the Phase 4B handoff's #1 priority direction).

## THE FAMILIES

### F1 — The Witnessed Letter (Cross-Glyph K ∩ خ + I'jam dot) — LEAD, rendered
- **Concept:** One angular glyph reads as both Latin **K** (stem + two arms) and Arabic **kha خ** (ascending/descending bowl), crowned by ONE positional dot at the **I'jam register** above the stem.
- **Construction logic:** weighty vertical stem (x124..156, y60..204), upper-arm triangle (stem→apex 232,44), lower-arm triangle (stem→apex 232,208), single 1.5u nuqta diamond above the stem at (140,28..52). 45°-lattice aligned. Monochrome.
- **Symbolic meaning:** the letter is *decided* by a single point — the same operation that makes خ (kha) from ح (ha) at the head of خاتم. The dot is simultaneously the I'jam (the point that makes the letter), the witness third-point, and the seal of the mark. Dot present = witnessed/attested; absent = unwitnessed letter.
- **Why it is distinctive:** not a container; a letterform-composite whose two readings (K and خ) share one silhouette ("same metal"); cannot be reached by "square + cut + dot."
- **Competitive risks (red team):** premise already published (Alkhafrah, non-competing); same genus as DigiCert/DocuSign letter-fusions; AI-reproducible skeleton. **Mitigation secured:** the dot reads ABOVE (graphemic I'jam), never in a check/tick position, so it does not collapse into the e-sign "letter+tick" cliché.
- **Cultural:** structural (the dot decides the first-letter's identity); passes the cultural test without ornament.
- **International:** reads as a bold abstract letter/monogram to non-Arabic viewers; the dot adds character even unknowing the name.
- **Small-size:** verified solid to 16px (stem mass carries; dot retains ~2px). Reverse-native, monochrome.
- **System potential:** high — the stem/arm/dot geometry seeds patterns, the dot drives the verification-state UI, the motion verb is the "witnessing reveal" (dot seats).
- **SVG:** `design/phase-4c/concepts/family-01-witnessed-letter.svg`

### F2 — The Sealing Cusp (closure by the glyph's own anatomy)
- **Concept:** completion = a stroke that closes by its own decisive cusp (ختم = to close/seal), no added container, no added dot.
- **Assessment:** honest, but as a *bare* geometric stroke it drifts toward generic swoosh/signature (the most saturated e-sign register). Deprioritized; the *closure* idea is better carried by F1's dot-seating and by motion than by a standalone cusp glyph. Weakest standalone of the letter-stroke set.

### F3 — The Third Point (Witness triad) — rendered
- **Concept:** two bold strokes (the two parties) converge to an apex resolved by ONE upper point (the sovereign witness). (Λ + witness dot.)
- **Construction:** two 45° wedges meeting at apex (128,84); dot above at (128,32..64).
- **Assessment:** clearly expresses the witness triad, but the "Λ + top dot" risks **mountain/placemark/location-pin** cliché and reads weakly as an identity; it is a diagram of an idea rather than an ownable form. Scored down. SVG: `family-03-third-point.svg`.

### F4 — The Registration Lock (attestation = exact register)
- **Concept:** two independent strokes falling into ONE point of exact register (the nuqta knot) — attestation as alignment.
- **Assessment:** strong philosophy, weak resting motif; off-alignment risk reads as print-error/glitch (Web3). Deprioritized as a resting mark; kept as a *document/verification* behavior.

### F5 — The Falling Seal-Tail (completion = descent)
- **Concept:** a bold vertical descender that "falls and seals" (the final م tail of ختم).
- **Assessment:** genuine word-root anchor and useful as a component, but a lone descending stroke is too thin/abstract alone. Kept as a secondary component (see word-derived doc), not a primary.

### F6 — The Pillar in the Stream (vertical-in-horizontal)
- **Concept:** an alif pillar interrupting/anchoring a horizontal stream (the Arabic horizontal vs Latin vertical tension).
- **Assessment:** symbolic but reads as a bar/dash glyph; weak ownability alone. Deprioritized.

### F7 — The Convergence Knot (vanishing-point witness) — rendered
- **Concept:** evidence edges stream to one off-center nuqta knot where all lines resolve — the observer/witness as the convergence of proof.
- **Construction:** three bold strokes converge asymmetrically at one off-center nuqta (164,124).
- **Assessment:** under-claimed register, but "edges converging to a point" risks network/spokes/AI-radiation cliché when rendered, and the off-center knot reads as a collision rather than a mark. SVG: `family-07-convergence-knot.svg`. Scored below F1.

### F8 — The Crown Register (the 1–2–0 dot density)
- **Concept:** the خ-ت crown-of-dots rhythm as a density record (one dot, two dots, none) — the word's numeric identity.
- **Assessment:** poetic and structurally Arabic, but a "dot field" is exactly the commodity register the lineage keeps dying in (grid/dot). Deprioritized; the dot-density idea survives only as a *pattern/subterranean* system element.

### F9 — The Neutral Cup I'jam toggle — rendered
- **Concept:** the bare kha/ha body twice — without its dot (unwitnessed ح, collapses) and with one positional dot (witnessed خ). Dot presence = the state.
- **Construction:** same bold body twice; only the one upper nuqta differs.
- **Assessment:** the clearest expression of the verification-state behavior, but a *diptych*, not a single mark — fails the single-silhouette requirement. Kept as the product **behavior proof** (the before/after of the verification state), not the mark. SVG: `family-09-neutral-cup.svg`.
