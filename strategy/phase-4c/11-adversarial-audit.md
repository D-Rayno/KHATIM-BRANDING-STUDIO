## The 17 Kill Questions

### 1. Is it generic?
**NO.** A cross-glyph K∩خ with an I'jam crown dot is not a stock geometric mark. It is a specific letterform construction.

### 2. Is it AI-looking?
**NO.** AI logo generators produce symmetric stars, gradient blobs, and "modern abstract geometric marks." F1's asymmetric arms and graphemic dot position are too specific for AI top-10 output.

### 3. Is it a geometric startup logo?
**NO.** It is a letterform, not a polygon container. The geometry serves a bilingual structural purpose, not a "we are modern and tech" signal.

### 4. Is it a cybersecurity logo?
**NO.** No shield, lock, key, hex, or chain. The mark reads as attestation/identity, not defense.

### 5. Is it a fintech logo?
**NO.** No interlocking blades, kinetic swash, or transaction nodes.

### 6. Is it a QR fragment?
**NO.** No lattice, grid, or pixel structure. The 45° angles are calligraphic/typographic, not digital-code.

### 7. Is it a seal cliché?
**NO.** No stamp face, badge, crest, wax seal, or circular rosette. The "impression" idea is carried by the letterform itself, not a container.

### 8. Is it an Arabic ornament cliché?
**NO.** No calligraphic swoosh, arabesque corners, or orientalist decoration. The Arabic influence is structural (I'jam dot position), not decorative.

### 9. Does the silhouette work at 16px?
**PASS.** Stem (4px) + arms (diagonal mass) + dot (~2px) are all visible. At favicon size the dot may reduce to 1px, but the K-silhouette remains distinctive.

### 10. Does it work in one color?
**PASS.** Monochrome native. `currentColor` throughout. Reverse-native.

### 11. Does it work without explanation?
**PASS.** The mark reads as a bold letterform with a distinctive crown dot. The Arabic reading is a bonus, not a requirement.

### 12. Can a competitor reproduce it quickly?
**PARTIAL RISK.** The *premise* (K+خ fusion) is AI-reproducible. The *exact geometry* (asymmetric arms, specific dot position, stem proportion) is protectable. The moat is the canonical construction + semantic argument, not the idea being rare. **Mitigation:** Register the exact geometric specification.

### 13. Does it actually express Impression?
**YES.** The dot is the impression — the single point that decides identity. The stem is the substrate. The arms are the parties. The dot seating = the irreversible mark.

### 14. Is the concept stronger than the execution?
**NO.** The execution carries the concept. The dot position is visible in the silhouette. The asymmetry is specific. This is not a "great story, weak drawing."

### 15. Does it remain credible for enterprise/government?
**YES.** Bold, exact, monochrome, letterform-based. No gradient, no mascot, no whimsy. Reads as infrastructure.

### 16. Does it remain culturally credible?
**YES.** The I'jam logic is genuine Arabic typography. Not tokenism. Not decoration. A deep observer recognizes the structural principle.

### 17. Does it remain future-proof?
**YES.** Letterforms outlast trend cycles. The K and خ are ancient. The dot position is canonical. The mark will not date.
