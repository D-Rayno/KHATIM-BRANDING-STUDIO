## Approved Facts

### Linguistic & Cultural

- Arabic root **ختم** (kh-t-m) = stamp/seal + complete/finish + reach the end **[S42][S43]**
- Historical Islamic seals functioned as identity/provenance evidence **[S44][S45][S46]**

### Regulatory & Market

- eIDAS 2 expands trust services to wallets, attestations, remote signing, archiving, ledgers **[S01][S02]**
- DocuSign rebranded to IAM (Intelligent Agreement Management) in 2024 **[S21][S22]**
- NIST 2025 integrates proofing, authentication, and federation

### Competitive

- Codegic markets a PKI/signing product suite under the exact name "Khatim" **[S38–S41]**
- Competitors converge on shields/locks/blue/"secure, compliant, fast, easy" **[S19][S23][S26]**
- 15 competitors mapped across 5 clusters:
  1. Agreement Workflow
  2. Digital Trust / Security
  3. European Trust
  4. Regional MENA
  5. Infrastructure / Embeddable

## Design Guardrails

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These guardrails govern all creative work. Violations require explicit strategic justification with evidence.

### Hierarchy Principles

1. Strategy outranks aesthetics
2. Distinctiveness outranks trendiness
3. System coherence outranks isolated visual novelty
4. Arabic and Latin identity must be designed as one system
5. Brand must scale beyond signing into broader digital trust
6. No generic cybersecurity, fintech, or SaaS conventions without explicit justification
7. Every irreversible design decision needs an evidence trail

### Visual Exclusions

8. ❌ No literal shields, locks, keys, checkmarks, pen strokes, circuit boards
9. ❌ No dark hacker aesthetic or neon gradients
10. ❌ No generic Islamic patterns or decorative arabesques
