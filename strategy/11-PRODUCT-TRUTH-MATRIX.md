## Conclusion for Brand Strategy
The brand cannot launch claiming to be a "Post-Quantum Federated Trust Network" (Long-term) when the product is a "PDF Digital Sealer" (V1). The brand messaging MUST focus on the **tamper-evident, witnessed nature of the V1 PDF seal**, using it as the foundational proof point for the eventual infrastructure.
