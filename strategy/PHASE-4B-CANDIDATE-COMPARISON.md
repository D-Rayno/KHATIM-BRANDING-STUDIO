## 2. Adversarial results (two independent non-builder agents)

Both agents were given the exact geometry and the locked strategic constraints, and were empowered to kill.

### Semiotics / Cultural Critic — blunt findings
- **Only C-B has genuinely structural Arabic/nuqta logic.** A fixed body with *one opened position* and a dot seated there = the nuqta operation (dot position determines the reading), the literal anatomy of **خ** (the base ح-body + one distinguishing dot). Not a decoration.
- **C-F fakes it hardest** — a purpose-built seat freezing the dot in one position is *inverse* nuqta logic, and reads as a gem-in-bezel (the forbidden jewelry/signet cliché) in real geometry.
- **C-A's center dot is decorative** (only one position, dead-center of a symmetric void); its **chamfered octagon is a brilliant-cut-gem silhouette** (jewelry adjacency).
- Ranking by genuine structural Arabic depth: **C-B > C-E > C-D > C-A > C-C > C-F**.
- Refinement offered for C-B: to fully own the **خ of خاتم** the decisive dot should sit *above* the body — a documented option, not a requirement (the diagonal-corner seat is what keeps C-B distinct from C-C's top-gate).

### Competitive Red Team — blunt findings
- **All six avoid the QR/lattice/PolyGrid/blockchain/node collision** (none is a repeated-module grid) — a genuine step out of the abandoned open-lattice register.
- **C-D and C-E still collide with the forbidden brother-register** (diagonal-offset / stacked-pane ≈ blockchain-node and DocuSign's diagonal-cell) — treat as blocked.
- **C-B is the strongest**: presence→absence via the cut, a **functional** (non-decorative) nuqta that completes the cut, real institutional mass, least-stereotyped gesture, no grid trigger.
- **C-F is the cleanest absence-lane fallback** (pure heavy deboss) but slips toward app-icon-with-dot; kept as secondary.
- **C-C weakest** (gateway/portal/fintech cliché, low authority). **C-E most generic** (offset overlapping squares = the default AI/SaaS/cyber template). **C-A generic seal / decorative dot.**
- C-B copyability: commodity as a *bare silhouette*, but the "dot completes the cut" relationship at a 2u/45° lattice is the most defensible single *form* in the set. System-level defense remains the real moat.

### Small-size / black-white verification (computed)
- Rendered isolated at 256→16px: C-B's ink profile is stable across sizes; the notch persists as an empty region and the seated **dot remains perceptible at 16px** (verified by pixel window around (0.656,0.656)). The square silhouette carries even if the dot were dropped.
- Reverses with zero edits (fill swaps → the corner reads differently, counter becomes ink).

## 4. Selection

- **PRIMARY — C-B "The Witnessed Corner."** Solid square seal (the khatim), three corners closed, the top-right corner opened by a single permanent subtraction, and a 45° nuqta seated in that opened corner completing it. It is the only candidate where the dot's position is *functional* (changes the reading), giving the **genuinely structural Arabic** the project has been claiming but not delivering. It is decisive, memorable, monochrome, reverse-native, small-size robust, system-scalable, and clear of every forbidden register.
- **SECONDARY — C-F "The Seated Nuqta."** The pure absence/deboss fallback, held as insurance (mirrors how Finalist B was held as the 16px insurance in Phase 3). Lower structural-Arabic claim, slightly more app-icon risk.
- **RESERVE — C-A** only if a future gate concludes the center-dot can be made structurally functional; as drawn it stays parked.

**Why C-B wins:** it is the single candidate that satisfies the hardest conditions *simultaneously* — a decisive ownable silhouette, a *true* displacement (presence→absence via the cut, not a fill), a **perceivable and functional** nuqta (the thing the previous iteration only captioned), competitive distance, small-size survival, and system potential — without triggering a single forbidden cliché or the QR/grid/node collision zone.
