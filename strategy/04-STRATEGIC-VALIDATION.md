# 04 Strategic Validation: Name Clearance

## Objective
Address the critical naming collision risk identified in Phase 1 (Codegic's "Khatim" product suite) by isolating knowns, unknowns, and required legal/business validations.

## 1. What is Known (Facts)
- **The Collision:** Codegic currently markets a product suite including *KhatimDoc, Khatim Sign Server, Khatim PKI Server, Khatim Timestamp Server, and Khatim Verification Server*.
- **Category Overlap:** Codegic's products exist in the exact same target category (Digital Trust/PKI/Signing) and fall under identical Nice Classes (9, 38, 42).
- **The Gap:** We currently possess no documented legal clearance, trademark search results, or co-existence agreements for the name "Khatim" in any jurisdiction.
- **The Implication:** Proceeding with visual identity design on an uncleared name introduces an existential risk of forced rebranding and wasted capital.

## 2. What is Uncertain (Hypotheses)
- **Active Trademark Status:** We do not know if Codegic holds *active, registered, and defensible* trademarks for "Khatim" in our primary target jurisdictions (e.g., EU, US, GCC).
- **Market Reality:** We do not know if Codegic's Khatim suite is actively sold and supported, or if it constitutes "vaporware" / parked intellectual property.
- **Co-existence Viability:** We do not know if a brand architecture utilizing a strong descriptor (e.g., "Khatim Trust") would be legally sufficient to avoid confusion, or if the core name is entirely radioactive.
- **Fallback Viability:** We do not know if semantic alternatives (Route C) can successfully carry the dual meaning of "seal" and "completion" in Arabic without losing the cultural moat.

## 3. What Must Be Validated (Action Plan)
To unblock Phase 2 (Visual Territory Exploration), the following must be executed immediately:

1. **Formal IP Search:** Engage intellectual property counsel to conduct a comprehensive trademark search for "Khatim" and related variants in Nice Classes 9, 38, 42 across primary launch jurisdictions.
2. **Commercial Audit:** Conduct a brief commercial audit of Codegic's "Khatim" products to determine market presence, active customer base, and litigiousness.
3. **Legal Conclusion:** Obtain a formal legal opinion on the viability of Route A (Keep Khatim) and Route B (Khatim + Descriptor).
4. **Fallback Vetting:** In parallel, generate and conduct preliminary semantic and trademark vetting on 3 alternative names (Route C) to ensure a viable backup exists if "Khatim" is rejected.
