## Product screens (Agent 06 — all three finalists share these behaviors)

1. **Signing screen:** deboss micro-displacement at the seal moment + tripartite Party/A/Witness/B strip. No theatrics.
2. **Verification screen:** bracketed monospace evidence block for a third-party verifier.
3. **Document status:** append-only bracketed lifecycle rail (draft→sent→viewed→sealed→witnessed→archived; dispute/revoked appended, never history-rewriting).
4. **Certificate:** tripartite print artifact; blind-emboss deboss; A/B/Witness information structure.
5. **Audit trail:** hash-chain forensic table; brackets; calm density.
6. **Success state:** "Sealed and witnessed." — a receipt, not a reward. No confetti.
7. **Tamper state:** alarm-by-structure, amber-oxide accent, no panic red.
8. **Dashboard:** records office, no metric noise.
9. **Mobile (375px):** evidence survives; ceremony compresses; haptic-seal micro-interaction candidate.

## System verdicts

- **Finalist A** — system-complete; the only finalist where grid, mark, pattern, icons, documents, UI, motion are one mechanism.
- **Finalist B** — strongest partial system at responsive/motion; must pair with C's layout discipline or A's grid.
- **Finalist C** — strongest layout generator; weakest mark economics; best paired with A or B.
