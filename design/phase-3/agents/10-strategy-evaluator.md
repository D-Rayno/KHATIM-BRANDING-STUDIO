## 0. How to use this instrument
1. Run knockouts (§3) first. Any KO = kill, regardless of weighted score.
2. Score survivors on the 10 criteria (§1) per the evidence tests (§2). Raw scale: 0 = fail, 1 = partial, 2 = pass.
3. Weighted total = Σ(weight × raw ÷ 2); max 100. Thresholds: ≥75 advance · 60–74.9 conditional (named risk to fix) · <60 park.
4. Apply finalist-diversity rules (§5) and compare findings against the baseline (§4).

## 2. Pass vs fail — testable evidence per criterion
1. **Differentiation** — PASS: zero shields/locks/keys/checkmarks/pen strokes/circuits/blue gradients AND no overlap with the competitor audit [S19][S23][S26]. FAIL: any rejected motif, or reads as an e-signature tool.
2. **Strategic fit** — PASS: primary mark encodes third-party attestation (witness/impression); heritage stays subterranean; evidence stays at product tier. FAIL: heritage overt, brackets at top level, or the three tiers weighted as equals.
3. **Memorability** — PASS: one ownable object identified in a 5-second silhouette test (no internal detail, no wordmark). FAIL: needs wordmark context or internal linework to be recognized.
4. **Credibility** — PASS: every implied claim (sealing, witnessing, permanence) maps to V1-real capability. FAIL: visually implies PQC, wallets, or a federated network (HYPOTHETICAL = vaporware cue).
5. **Cultural relevance** — PASS: heritage carried by structure (45° nuqta proportions, calligraphic geometry) with zero decoration. FAIL: arabesque, literal wax seal, calligraphic flourish, generic Islamic pattern.
6. **International scalability** — PASS: reads "official/institutional" without language; glyph still works under fallback name (e.g., "Nexus Trust"). FAIL: depends on Arabic calligraphy legibility or English wordplay.
7. **Future expansion** — PASS: geometry admits extension (nodes, registers, attestations, ledgers) without redesign. FAIL: depicts a document-specific or signature-specific act.
8. **Digital usability** — PASS: resolves at 16px favicon / 24px app icon in 1-color monochrome. FAIL: relies on gradient, shadow, or fine detail that collapses at small size.
9. **Defensibility** — PASS: structure carries meaning without the name "Khatim" AND is trademark-distinct. FAIL: meaning collapses under naming Routes B/C/D, or geometry is generic enough to collide with Codegic marks.
10. **System potential** — PASS: the mark generates layout/motion/UI rules (grid, brackets, state transitions) consistently. FAIL: a freestanding glyph with no governing logic.

## 3. Knockout rules (auto-kill on any hit)
- **K1** Fails 16px monochrome legibility → kill.
- **K2** Cybersecurity cliché (shield/lock/key/checkmark/circuit/blue gradient/neon) → kill (D-004).
- **K3** Name-dependent (meaning requires "Khatim" letterform/wordplay) → kill — P-001 unresolved.
- **K4** Cultural decoration (wax seal, arabesque, Islamic pattern, calligraphic flourish) → kill (D-003 guardrail).
- **K5** Vaporware cue (implies PQC / federated network / wallets) → kill (11 truth matrix).
- **K6** Hierarchy inversion (heritage overt, or brackets in the brand tier, or tiers as equals) → kill (locked hierarchy).
- **K7** Litigious/forensic aggression at top level (red warning labels, harsh terminal aesthetics) → kill (explicitly NOT per 08).

## 5. Finalist selection — meaningful difference, not cosmetic variants
- **R1 Mechanism split:** the 3 finalists must map to distinct semiotic mechanisms: (A) relief/impression physics (positive↔negative displacement), (B) relational triadic geometry (A+B+Witness), (C) structural proportion system (45° nuqta made ownable). Same mechanism in a new stroke/orientation = one slot, one finalist.
- **R2 Family ranking:** run K1–K7, then score; rank candidates inside each mechanism family; best-of-family advances to the final three.
- **R3 Coverage swap:** if the top two weighted scores share a mechanism, swap the lower out for the best candidate of a missing mechanism family.
- **R4 Distinct systems:** each finalist must pair its primary mechanism with a different supporting behavior (subterranean grid / tripartite layout / product-tier brackets), so system architecture differs — not just the glyph.
- **R5 Name-independence retest:** all three finalists re-verified under a fallback name ("Nexus Trust") before any gate decision.
