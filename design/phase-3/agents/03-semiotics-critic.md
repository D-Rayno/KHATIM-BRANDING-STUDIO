## 1. Method & Semiotic Framework

Each family is tested on four axes; verdicts are **PASSES / SURVIVES / FAILS**.

1. **Denotative sufficiency (the "mime test").** Stripped of its caption, does the form *produce* the intended meaning (witness, imprint, completion, proof), or merely point at a story that produces it? Most geometric marks are captions with geometry attached.
2. **Connotative safety.** Does the form activate forbidden sign-fields (decorative Arabic, Islamic ornament, orientalism, wax, jewelry, shields/locks/UI chrome) even unintentionally?
3. **Structural heritage test.** Heritage is legitimate only when *structural* (the 45° *nuqta* grid as construction discipline), never as *costume* (rosettes, calligraphy, gemstone faceting).
4. **Hierarchy containment.** PRIMARY = witness/deboss; SECONDARY = subterranean grid; PRODUCT = forensic UI. A family importing the forensic layer into the master brand violates the tiers however handsome the form.

## 3. Cultural-Depth Audit (خاتم / ختم)

The root ختم carries seal + closure + completion + finality. The audit asks: does any family resolve this depth **without costume**? Two do. **F-01** embeds the *nuqta* — the rhombic dot-unit of Arabic calligraphy — as the construction grid itself: heritage as engineering, invisible as ornament, the only legitimate mode of cultural depth here. **F-05** echoes the carved seal matrix (mass/void) structurally, without depicting artifact.

Descending into orientalist shorthand: **F-09** (radial symmetry → rosette = decorative Islamic cliché) and **F-10** (gemstone faceting → jewelry tokenism). **F-04** fails differently — not orientalism but *heritage misallocation*: Roman masonry standing in for the brand's own root. The study's elimination list (rings, wax, calligraphic خاتم, eye) was semiotically sound; my audit confirms, not defers. Name-independence is the real tokenism test: all survivors function under "Nexus Trust" — none needs Arabic script or the name to mean. They pass.

## 4. Independent Ranking by Symbolic Integrity

1. **F-01 — Pressed Nuqta.** Passes outright: meaning, permanence, structural heritage in one gesture.
2. **F-02 — Matrix & Impression.** The most semiotically complete family — it encodes the *relationship*, which F-05 cannot. Demoted below F-01 on identifiability cost, not meaning; the designer inverted these, I correct on semantic grounds.
3. **F-05 — Void Core.** Strongest form; must import causation from system behavior. Survives, narrowly.
4. **F-15 — Axis Witness.** Best rendering of triadic witness; survives despite symmetry-as-balance risk and bracket adjacency.
5. **F-07 — Registration Notch.** Highest-variance survivor; the damaged-vs-measured read is unresolved.

**Reserve, re-tiered:** F-11 (texture layer), F-14 (forensic UI layer). **Rejected:** F-03, F-04, F-06, F-08, F-09, F-10, F-12, F-13, F-16.

- **F-03:** convergence is too generic to specify witness; mime, not meaning.

## 7. What a Symbol Must Still Prove in Later Phases

1. **Human reading test.** Unprompted buyer-panel reading before the narrative is heard — the mime test executed on real people, not assumed.
2. **16px difference-legibility.** F-01's pressed-vs-unpressed cell and F-07's notch must survive favicon reduction under real rendering, not mockups.
3. **Motion semantics.** The deboss families (F-01/F-02/F-05) may require a temporal "press" event to supply causation — prove it in motion or accept muteness.
4. **Trademark/novelty clearance.** Dot-grids, octagons, chevrons, and notched bars are occupied territory; symbolic integrity is meaningless without legal distinctiveness.
5. **Anti-ornament guarantee.** Re-verify at every convergence: no rosette (F-09 precedent), no gemstone (F-10 precedent), no wax, no costume — heritage remains structural or the family dies.
6. **Hierarchy discipline.** F-11 and F-14 redeploy only at pattern/forensic tiers; the master brand must not be recolonized by the product layer.
