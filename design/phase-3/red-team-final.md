| # | Attack | Result | One-line reason |

| 2 | "Just another premium SaaS brand" | SURVIVES | Hard monochrome lattice rejects the kinetic-blob/rounded SaaS dialect outright. |
| 3 | "Just another Arabic-inspired identity" | SURVIVES | The nuqta grid is construction, not ornament — the only legitimate cultural mode (Agent 03); watch: discipline slip → generic tessellation. |
| 4 | "Just another seal" | SURVIVES | No signet ring, no wax, no hallmark face — the pressed cell is physics, not stamp iconography. |
| 5 | "Visually impressive, strategically empty" | SURVIVES | The strategy is embedded in the form: the one altered unit *is* the attestation event, not decoration rehearsed after the fact. |

## FINALIST B — THE VOID CORE (from F-05)

Geometry on file: one solid square silhouette in evenodd fill with a single centered diamond counter — mass interrupted by pure absence.

| # | Attack | Result | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Just another cybersecurity brand" | SURVIVES | Zero cyber vocabulary — no shields, locks, hexes, or glow. |
| 2 | "Just another premium SaaS brand" | **FAILS** | "Solid square + centered hole" is premium-SaaS minimalism's default dialect; this is the most exposed finalist to that specific attack. |
| 3 | "Just another Arabic-inspired identity" | SURVIVES | Abstract mass/void; zero ornamental load, zero cultural drift. |
| 4 | "Just another seal" | **FAILS** | The mass/void structure is the carved-stamp abstraction by its own admission — a square face with centered counter drifts toward square-seal (inkan/hanko) adjacency; literal-stamp territory the gate already rejected. |
| 5 | "Visually impressive, strategically empty" | **FAILS** | As a static glyph it asserts nothing — causation must be rented from motion and application behavior; Agent 03's own "narrowly survives" relied on that rental. |
| 6 | "Too abstract to be memorable" | SURVIVES | Reduction maximizes reproducible memorability even as it minimizes meaning; a square with one void is drawable from memory. |
| 7 | "Easy for Adobe/DocuSign to copy" | **FAILS** | A single silhouette with a centered rhombic counter is the most imitable geometry on record; registration is defensible only through consistent system use — the weakest moat of the three. |
| 8 | "Will not work at 16px" | SURVIVES | The 16px benchmark of the program (8px hole, 4px walls on the specimen); reverses with zero edits. |

**Verdict: KILL** (as a standalone finalist). The form is elegant but it is *correct* only on proof 8; it fails the existential attacks (copyability, strategic emptiness, SaaS-dialect, stamp-adjacency) that decide a primary identity. Its correct role is as an asset: the sub-32px/responsive derivative for whichever direction proceeds — not a convergence candidate.

**Severity:** eliminated from CD convergence; retained as (a) favicon/mobile fallback and (b) insurance form in the event A's reconstruction collapses. No Phase-4 work permitted beyond documenting that asset role.

## FINALIST C — THE AXIS WITNESS (from F-15)

Geometry on file: two solid vertical masses (Party A at x=170–260, Party B at x=380–470) flanking a central elongated rhombus (the Witness, x=270–370); diagonals corrected to dx=dy (true 45°) in this study.

| # | Attack | Result | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Just another cybersecurity brand" | SURVIVES | No cyber vocabulary; composition, not icon. |
| 2 | "Just another premium SaaS brand" | **FAILS** | A mirror-symmetric bar+rhombus monogram is the generic abstract-glyph dialect the category defaults to; specificity evaporates at silhouette level. |
| 3 | "Just another Arabic-inspired identity" | SURVIVES | The rhombus is a construction member, not a motif; no ornament carried. |
| 4 | "Just another seal" | SURVIVES | Flanked masses with a central third evade the stamp-face trope better than B. |
| 5 | "Visually impressive, strategically empty" | SURVIVES | The strongest strategy encoding of the three — the triadic relation lives in the structure, not in a slogan. |
| 6 | "Too abstract to be memorable" | SURVIVES | Party / Witness / Party is the most narratable form on file; "symmetry = impartiality" is at least a story. |
| 7 | "Easy for Adobe/DocuSign to copy" | **FAILS** | Two bars + center rhombus is a freely recomposable configuration; bracket/code-adjacency risk makes the copy bar lowest-common-denominator (Agent 08's own conditional caveat). |
| 8 | "Will not work at 16px" | **FAILS** | Three solid elements separated by hairline gaps (10-unit class on the study) cannot resolve at 16px; the grammar's single-decisive-silhouette rule (rule 4) is violated by construction. |

**Verdict: KILL** (as a mark). It encodes the strategy better than anything here and still dies, because a brand mark cannot be a multi-element diagram at 16px and cannot defend a recomposable symmetric configuration. Agent 02's demotion ("illustrates the role, not the ownable object") is confirmed by geometry, not by taste.

**Severity:** eliminated from convergence; its correct remanence is the tripartite A/Witness/B layout discipline for certificates and verification hierarchy — a system rule, not a mark. Fixing the grid (dx=dy) has already happened here and it still fails; the failure is economic, not geometric.
