## Verdict line

## 1. PREFERRED DIRECTION — A: THE DISPLACEMENT EVENT

The 45° rhombic nuqta lattice in which exactly one cell is pressed solid — attestation as a
single permanent state-change against a uniform field. It wins on all nine selection dimensions:

1. **Meaning.** The strategy is embedded in the form, not rehearsed after it: the one altered
   unit *is* the attestation event. It is the only finalist unifying all three locked tiers —
   deboss (PRIMARY), nuqta grid (SECONDARY), state-change logic (PRODUCT) — in one gesture.
   It directly encodes the Sovereign Witness's core claim: a dyadic action becomes a triadic,
   irreversible state.
2. **Distinctiveness.** It speaks no category dialect: not cyber (no shield/lock/hex), not SaaS
   (no kinetic blob/rounding), not seal iconography (no signet/wax/hallmark face). The only
   residual adjacency — the pixel/data-grid trope — is controlled by the 45°-rhombic discipline.
3. **Credibility.** Permanence physics + measurement language is the anti-list's approved route
   to authority without institutional costume. A pressed cell is a fact, not a claim; the mark
   never asserts, it records.
4. **Memorability.** "One difference in a uniform field" is a graspable, retellable human idea —
   the strongest concept-level hook on file (red team attack 6: SURVIVES).
5. **Cultural depth.** The nuqta grid is *construction, not ornament* — the only legitimate
   heritage mode per the semiotic audit. Heritage lives invisibly in the metrology: the cultural
   moat with zero orientalism risk.
6. **International viability.** Name-agnostic (passes the Nexus Trust fallback). Pressure and
   impression are universal physics — wax, inkan, khatim — legible across cultures with no
   decorative load to misread.
7. **System potential.** The only finalist that passed all 10 application checks natively and all
   twelve system dimensions: grid, mark, pattern, icons, documents, UI, and motion ("the press")
   are one mechanism. B and C are partial systems; A is a complete one.
8. **Future-proofing.** A system of one verb (press) scales from favicon to building facade and
   from document signing to AI-agent attestation and supply-chain ledgers — the Witness's
   expansion territory — without new vocabulary. Monochrome-native; no trend-dependent styling.
9. **Defensibility.** Weakest dimension — the red team is right that the form-level delta (one
   solid unit among hollow ones) is imitable. The defense is therefore re-anchored from form to
   system: the registered "press" motion, the die/impression behavior (F-02), and the documented
   grid discipline. Copying A requires adopting the logic, not just the look; that is the moat,
   and Phase 4 must argue it at system level only.

No other finalist survives even half of these dimensions (B fails 4 of 8 red-team attacks; C
fails 3). A wins because meaning, culture, and system are the same object in it alone.

## 2. SECONDARY DIRECTION — B: THE VOID CORE

Red-team verdict was KILL as a standalone mark, and this convergence ratifies that kill: a solid
square with a centered counter is premium-SaaS minimalism's default dialect, drifts toward
square-seal (inkan/hanko) adjacency, is strategically mute without rented motion, and carries
the weakest copyability moat on record. It stays alive for exactly one reason: it is the 16px
benchmark of the entire program (8px hole, 4px walls; reverses with zero edits; lowest
implementation complexity of all finalists). Its retained roles, per the red team:

- **Sub-32px derivative:** favicon, mobile icon, and small-size fallback for Direction A.
- **Insurance form:** if A's reconstruction fails its re-audit, B is the designated fallback
  primary — but only after its SaaS-dialect and stamp-adjacency failures are explicitly
  mitigated. No Phase-4 work on B beyond documenting this asset role.

## 3. KILLED DIRECTION — C: THE AXIS WITNESS

Killed as a mark, despite encoding the strategy better than anything on file. The reasons are
economic, not geometric (the dx=dy grid fix was already applied and it still fails): a
three-element diagram with hairline gaps cannot resolve at 16px — violating the grammar's
single-decisive-silhouette rule by construction — and a symmetric bar+rhombus configuration is
freely recomposable, making it undefendable and generically SaaS at silhouette level. It
illustrates the role rather than owning the object.

**What survives:** the tripartite Party-A / Witness / Party-B discipline is dissolved into a
binding layout rule for certificates, verification hierarchy, and the signing-screen strip. Its
narrative value ("symmetry = impartiality") transfers to buyer-facing decks and verification
screens in every direction. C dies as a candidate and lives as infrastructure.

## 4. CONDITIONS ON THE PREFERRED (red-team REFINE terms — binding on Phase 4)

1. **BLOCKER — construction:** re-draw A's geometry per the binding vector spec at a 16px-proof
   scale (stroke s = u/2, gaps ≥ u, counters ≥ 2u; scale compensation below 32px) and re-audit.
   The current SVG (0.33px strokes at 16px) does not proceed.
2. **Fallback trigger:** if the reconstruction fails re-audit, adopt B's silhouette as the formal
   sub-32px substitute — or, if A is unrecoverable, invert to B-as-primary with its named
   failures mitigated before Phase 4 entry. No finalist proceeds as currently drawn.
3. **NON-BLOCKER — defensibility:** anchor all exclusivity claims at system level (registered
   "press" motion, F-02 die/impression behavior, grid-discipline documentation). Phase 4
   reasoning must not claim form-level exclusivity.
4. **Rendering discipline:** geometry-first rendering only (displaced geometry + physical
   light/shadow spec: light angle, falloff, scale thresholds). No emboss filters, no texture
   clip-art — the skeuomorphism YES-block stands until the spec passes.
5. **Drift watch:** 45°-vs-square discipline must resolve at 16px to keep the data-grid trope
   closed; any visible grid line that is not load-bearing is a kill (grammar rule 2).
