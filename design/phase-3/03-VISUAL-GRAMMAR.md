# 03 — VISUAL GRAMMAR (SYNTHESIS)

> **Label: EXPLORATION — NOT FINAL**
> The visual grammar synthesized from Agent 01 (Creative Director), Agent 05 (Brand Systems), Agent 07 (Vector). These are mechanism rules, not locked identity.

## 1. The six rules of the emerging grammar (Agent 05)

1. **State-change is the brand's only verb.** Before/after; unpressed/pressed; the press is the single permitted motion narrative.
2. **The nuqta grid is invisible proportion, never decoration.** If a grid line is visible, it must be the load-bearing skeleton of a symbol — never a surface pattern. (Anti-drift: generic Islamic tessellation = instant kill.)
3. **Evidentiary brackets and forensic monospace live only in the product tier.** Their gravity informs the brand; their glyphs do not.
4. **One decisive silhouette per candidate.** Every mark must collapse to a single readable shape (Agent 07's construction rule).
5. **Geometry-first rendering.** Deboss = displaced geometry + physical light/shadow; Photoshop emboss filters, texture clip-art banned (Agent 01 §9 mitigation).
6. **One gesture per surface.** Any application needing more than one idea to read is off-system.

## 2. Construction module (Agent 07 — binding spec for all synthesis SVG)

- Base unit **u**; nuqta rhombus u × u; lattice pitch 2u; stroke s = u/2; counters ≥ 2u; gaps ≥ u.
- Ratios: multiples of u in the 1/2/3/5/8 sequence.
- Optical corrections: overshoot u/16–u/8; diagonal thickening ×1.07; scale compensation below 32px.
- 16px rule: only single-silhouette, counter-driven marks survive unmodified.

## 3. Anti-lists (locked guardrails — Agent 01 §4)

Never feel like: cybersecurity (shields, locks, checkmarks, hex, hacker glow), SaaS friendliness (rounded cheerful type, kinetic blobs, neon), institutional costume (columns, crests, engraved bank pastiche), heritage kitsch (arabesque, signets, wax seals, orientalism), litigious aggression (panic red, forensic paranoia at brand level), crypto/Web3 slang, cheap skeuomorphism, the literal witness (eyes, figures, notary scenes).

## 4. Mechanisms for the three "hows" (Agent 01 §6–8)

- **Authority without institutional cliché:** permanence physics, measurement language, triadic composition, restraint discipline, typographic severity (forensic mono quarantined).
- **Heritage without ornament:** nuqta as metrology, rigor over flourish, universal physics, name independence.
- **Cryptographic trust without cyber clichés:** key metaphor = matrix/deformation pair, state-change as proof visual, evidence quarantined, weight over glow, zero security theater.

## 5. Failure-mode watchlist (from Agents 01, 03, 09)

Skeuomorphism drift · jewelry/object drift · pattern drift · courthouse drift · archive-equals-past drift · literal-witness drift (prohibited outright) · over-quiet collapse at favicon scale · gate-breach (exploration hardening into locks).
