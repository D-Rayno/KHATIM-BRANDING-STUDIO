## 1. Concept: The Impression

The mark is a chamfered octagon resting on the 45° rhombic nuqta lattice. At its center sits a rhombic void — the **impression** — and at the void's optical center, a single perceivable nuqta — the **attestation**.

Three things are happening at once, and this is deliberate:

| Element | Role | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Substrate (octagon)** | the matrix | the underlying, unmarked surface of reality |
| **Rhombic void** | the impression | the mark the press leaves; a displacement of the surface it touches |
| **Nuqta at center** | the attestation | the single point that confirms the mark was made — the witness's dot |

The story the mark tells, in one sentence: **a lasting mark is left by a verified press — the displacement *is* the proof, and the dot at its center is the witness.**

This is the displacement-event idea the takeover audit told us to preserve, now structurally repaired: bounded silhouette, decisive counter, single perceivable detail, real negative space, a mark that survives at 16px from its own construction.

---

## 2. System logic: one module, many states

The identity is **one geometry, understood as a family of states**. All states share the same 8u × 8u module:

| State | Construction | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **matrix** | octagon only (substrate, no void) | the un-pressed / pre-verification surface |
| **impression** | octagon + rhombic void | the pressed surface, pre-witness |
| **attested** | octagon + void + nuqta (the master) | the verified, permanent record |
| **field** | a 45° lattice of units in mixed states | the pattern / system surface |

The identity is not "a logo." It is **a system in which states are meaningful**. A document enters the system as a matrix, is pressed into an impression, and is attested into permanence. The mark is the atom; the system is the states it can exist in.

## 3. Typography pairing (development placeholder)

The final typefaces are **not licensed / not locked** (per brief). The pairing logic below is locked; the exact faces are candidates to be resolved.

**Latin:** severe grotesque, all-caps KHATIM, +2–6% tracking, bold-to-black weights. Cap height calibrates to the 8u module: wordmark cap height = 5u so the wordmark sits on the same baseline plane as the mark's lattice.

**Arabic:** modern sans خاتم, alif height = Latin cap height (5u), shared baseline, rounded-to-flat terminals balanced against the Latin. Arabic is **never** smaller than Latin; the two pair and mirror per market (LTR/RTL), never translate each other's script.

**System font fallbacks** used in the current SVG wordmark/lockup files: `Helvetica Neue`/`Arial` for Latin; `Tahoma`/`Segoe UI`/`Geeza Pro` for Arabic. **These are placeholders** for the licensed final faces. They are marked `DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE / NOT FINAL` and must not ship as final.

## 4. Color rationale

Per `02-COLOR-CANDIDATE-SYSTEM.md`, the rebuilt identity is **monochrome-first**. The mark carries meaning in geometry, never color. Any single ink works: black on white, white on black, one color on any substrate. This is not a limitation — it is the identity's honesty: a trust mark cannot depend on a color to carry its weight.

---

## 5. The single verb: the press

The identity has exactly one motion: **the press** — the transition from matrix (emptiness) to impression (displacement) to attested (permanence). Every interface transition ends in a permanent state. No fade, no slide, no bounce, no spring. The system's motion grammar inherits directly from its geometry: a unit's state changes, and the change is instantaneous and irreversible, exactly like a real press.
