# 04 — Application Prototypes

**Status: PROVISIONAL / SYSTEM TESTING**

Vector application sheet: `svg/khatim-application-system.svg`

## Surfaces tested

### Website
The identity leads with the state-change concept without relying on a shield, lock, key, checkmark or blue gradient.

### Verification
Evidence brackets and restrained data typography communicate proof as product behavior. They are not part of the primary logo.

### Certificate
The tripartite layout rule is expressed as hierarchy rather than as a three-part logo.

### Mobile
The compact mark is allowed to replace the full field when screen real estate cannot support the master construction.

### Motion
The only proposed brand motion is displacement: a unit moves/changes state and settles. No celebratory particles, blobs or cybersecurity effects.
